# Recap Follow-Through - a recap is written at the moment of promising

A recap freezes when nothing has been done yet, so it is the worst record of whether a commitment was honoured. The 2026-08-20 sweep evidence: `research/dev-workflows/2649-rules-history/archive/recap-followthrough.md`.

1. **Every recap carries a follow-through block: who owes what, to whom, by when** - a named person on each side of each item.
2. **A commitment to someone outside ZAO goes on the BOARD**, not only in a doc: route=human, real due date.
3. **A doc whose last-validated date predates its own deadlines is not current state.** Re-validate before citing it.
4. **Distinguish BLOCKED from DROPPED in the doc** - write the precondition next to the promise.
5. **Do not resolve an ambiguous promise by guessing** - record it as UNRESOLVED.

Guards: one block per recap, not a status on every line. A recap complete on the day says so. Re-validating = append a dated follow-through note; never edit the original record of what was said.
