# Base Rooms: Telegram export research

**Scope.** Read-only review of the Telegram Desktop export at `ChatExport_2026-11-16`, focused on its `Baserooms` chat. The HTML parts run from **2026-09-06 18:08:25 to 2026-11-16 18:06:46** (export timestamps marked `UTC-05:00`). Messages below are cited by file and Telegram message ID. Quotes are intentionally short. No attachments were needed.

## Direct evidence

- **What it is in this export:** “Base Room” resolves to **Base Rooms / Baserooms**, a Base-chain community and crypto project using token ticker **BRTC**, with a website/dashboard at `baserooms.io`. On 6 Sep the operator described it as “my initiative” and a “passion project for the community” (messages.html, **2026-09-06**, #64); the dashboard “Rigs” were announced running later that evening (#438). A buy-alert post identifies the BRTC token and Base Uniswap pools (messages.html, #466).
- **Launch and initial premise:** before launch, the operator said they would be “mining mstr on base” and that holder growth increased MSTR mined (messages.html, **2026-09-06**, #21; #62). The operator later clarified: “Its not mining” literally; it was a meme referencing early Bitcoin mining, and “We ‘mine’ through trading activity and growing our community” (messages3.html, **2026-09-22**, #2786, #2790).
- **Staking and rewards:** on **2026-09-07**, the operator announced “staking is live” and said users could stake BRTC to earn MSTR (messages2.html, #1271). In the same chat, the operator described connecting a wallet, selecting an amount of BRTC to stake, and earning a share of the then-described 3 MSTR pool (#1570). On **2026-09-24**, they said users “gotta stake it for now” to earn; holding alone was not enough (messages3.html, #3218). A snapshot before staking for an airdrop was announced **2026-09-08** (messages2.html, #1855); the export does not establish that this snapshot led to an airdrop.
- **Product expansion announced in chat:** on **2026-09-12**, a message said users could swap and stake directly on the site (messages2.html, #2248). On **2026-09-22**, the operator described three assets in a pool, three more planned for December, forthcoming launchpad functions, a proposed 20% share of launched-coin fees, and 1% trading fees in MSTR (messages3.html, #2680–#2682). On **2026-09-25**, messages announced a “room factory,” a launchpad feature, and games/music in “baserooms os” (messages3.html, #3356, #3416, #3437). A desktop buddy was announced **2026-09-26** (#3637); mobile installation was mentioned **2026-10-19** (messages6.html, #11270).
- **Positioning and open product clarity:** a member asked what rooms and the mining mechanics were on **2026-09-22** (messages3.html, #2785); the operator answered with the metaphor and community/trading explanation above. On **2026-09-24**, after a member said the platform was confusing, the operator acknowledged: “i went too much on the MEME part” without explaining what it was (#3177, #3179). On **2026-10-02**, the operator called it “the first prediction market on YAPS” (messages4.html, #5048); the export does not define that feature.

## Inference

- The chat presents Base Rooms as a mix of a community/social space and a Base-chain token/staking app. The “rooms” appear to be both community metaphors and product chat/voice spaces; the export does not give a complete room mechanic or a definitive product specification.
- “MSTR” rewards, fee shares, pool integrations, launchpad functions, and other roadmap items are statements by participants in the chat. This export alone does not verify contracts, reward funding, delivery, or current status.
- Case-insensitive searches across all eight HTML parts found no occurrences of **Fractal**, **TCG**, or **WaveWarZ**. The export therefore provides no supported connection to those names.
