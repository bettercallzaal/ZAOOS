The grill learned to filter itself today.

When ZOE launched the proactive grill loop Saturday, it surfaced everything — all 96 open items at once. That's not an agent, that's a spreadsheet. Sunday's work fixed it: the grill now shows the top few, not 96. Each card explains itself — what it is, why it's there, what action closes it. And when something enters the capture flow (a link, a voice note, a forwarded message), the grill shows the original context alongside it. You see what you sent AND why it mattered when you sent it.

Four PRs, one principle: one clear thing at a time, with enough context to act, and a visible path to close it. The week-grill version goes further — ZOE walks you through today's agenda one item at a time, events first, then code. It's less like checking a dashboard and more like being briefed by someone who knows what matters. That's the direction. The organism that does less, cleanly, is more powerful than the one that shows you everything and asks you to sort it yourself.
