Two things are converging. Proof Drops plus the endowment spec is one organism telling the world what it built and getting funded for it. That's a different product than a fan community. That's infrastructure for a creator economy that works.

The intention for Wednesday: name one more thing that already exists but hasn't been written down yet. The organism is full of those. That's the work right now — not building new organs, but recognizing the ones we didn't know we had.
