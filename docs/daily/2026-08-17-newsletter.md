## MINDFUL MOMENT

There's a difference between a decision that's made and a decision that's recorded. Fractal tonight will make a decision about DreamNet Phase 0. The moment it happens in conversation, it exists — but only in the memories of the people in that call, which is the most fragile storage medium we have. The act of writing it into a numbered research doc is not bureaucracy. It's how the decision survives the meeting and becomes something you can build from next week, next month, next year.

The Year of the ZABAL runs on provable outputs. An unrecorded decision is a claim. A doc is a receipt.
