## MINDFUL MOMENT

H2 midpoint is a strange thing. The calendar says you're exactly halfway, but the work rarely distributes that way — some weeks are worth a month, some weeks disappear. What actually marks the midpoint isn't the date, it's the willingness to look honestly at the scoreboard and not flinch.

The builds that are ahead: let them run. The ones that are behind: decide now whether they're worth accelerating or worth officially deprioritizing. A project that's "in progress but slow" indefinitely is a tax on attention. An officially-paused project costs nothing until you pick it back up. There's a version of today where the most productive thing is naming two things you're not building in H2 and freeing up the focus for what you are.

Year of the ZABAL means finishing what's worth finishing. The midpoint is where you find out which things are worth it.
