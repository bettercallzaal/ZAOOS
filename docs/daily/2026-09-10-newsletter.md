## MINDFUL MOMENT

Doc 2478 is about inverted alarms — systems that go quiet when they should be loud. The failure isn't a false positive; it's an absence that reads as normal.

There's a version of this in the deal memo situation. The memos are written. The system looks complete. The signal that something is still broken is the silence on the other end — no signed contract, no reply, no confirmation. A written memo that hasn't landed is the same as a signal that stopped firing. The system believes it sent; the other side never received.

Thursday's intention: send something that produces a response. Not just output — a closed loop.
