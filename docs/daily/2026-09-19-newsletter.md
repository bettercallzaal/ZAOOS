The sponsor email isn't just overdue; it's Day 20 of silence from someone who agreed to receive it. The heartbeat is the send. Send it and the signal exists. Don't, and the pipeline stays invisible, healthy on paper, doing nothing.

Build the heartbeat. Send the thing.
