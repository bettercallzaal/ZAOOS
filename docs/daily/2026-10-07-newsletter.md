## Draft

Doc 2600 shipped today. When I started numbering research documents, I did not know what 2600 would look like. Now it's a synthesis — the corpus talking about itself, mapping what's been learned, naming the gaps. Two thousand six hundred bets that writing something down would matter later. The batting average is high enough that I'm not stopping.

Two features shipped alongside it. zaoos.com is now an actual operations center — cockpit, shell, command palette. It was a landing page. Now it's a desk. And ZAO OS picked up a native signer, Spotify, realtime chat, and a ZOE control plane in one PR. The control plane piece is the one I keep thinking about: ZOE now has a surface inside the main app, not just in Telegram. That changes what's possible with the agent layer.

The research today was almost entirely Base/Web3 music infrastructure — twenty docs, one sprint. NFC wristbands with ZK proof-of-presence, EAS scrobble attestations for fan badges, Story Protocol for sample clearance, ERC-6551 Token Bound Accounts living inside album NFTs, Sablier streaming escrow for artist grants. The individual pieces keep landing. At some point soon, I'll start building the integrations between them and we'll see what the stack actually looks like assembled. That's the next phase of WaveWarZ.

**MINDFUL MOMENT**

Doc 2600 is a milestone but it's also just a number. The thing that matters is that 2,600 questions got asked and answered — in writing, with sources, with evidence. The act of asking the question carefully enough to write it down is what makes the answer useful six months later.

There's a practice in there that goes beyond research docs. Every decision that got written down with a why is a decision I can revisit without starting from scratch. Every correction that got timestamped and sourced is a record of the system improving itself. The year of the ZABAL is partly about shipping — but it's also about building institutional memory that doesn't evaporate when a session ends.

The next 400 docs will be different from the first 400. The library knows what it knows. Now it can start asking harder questions.
