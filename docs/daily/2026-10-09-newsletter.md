**MINDFUL MOMENT**

Doc 2636 asks what it looks like when an organization watches itself work. The answer is: five
changes. Not a hundred. Not a zero. Five specific things that came up when the measurement was
honest about what was happening.

That's the discipline that matters more than any individual build. Knowing what to stop, what to
change, what to do next — from evidence, not from the story you'd prefer to tell. The Year of
the ZABAL is about building fast, but building fast on a wrong foundation isn't speed, it's
drift. Five changes from one honest day of looking is the thing that keeps the foundation solid.

The North Creek sprint today is the same principle from a different angle. Three docs before a
single line of artist-page code. Know what you're building, know who it's for, know what it
should look like. Then build.
