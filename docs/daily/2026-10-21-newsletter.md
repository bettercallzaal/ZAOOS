**Subject: The map is drawn. Now we build.**

---

Yesterday felt like one of those days where a lot of the fog cleared at once.

We shipped the ZAOOS graduation map -- doc 2691, now on revision 3 with Zaal's rulings locked in. This is the clearest picture we've ever had of what ZAOOS is, what stays here as the permanent lab, and what earns its own repo when it's ready. ZAOstock graduated. COC Concertz graduated. The ZAO cards pipeline is next in line -- and that work is already moving. Phase A of the card builder shipped yesterday: pure logic, pure tests, zero UI. The foundation is real before the facade goes up. That's the way.

The ZAO TCG Card is taking shape too. We dropped a read-only WaveZ Station buyer snapshot into the repo -- the first real data behind what the first ZAO card represents. 188 holders. Real people. A real community that will carry a card with the ZAO name on it. That's not a feature. That's a moment.

---

**MINDFUL MOMENT**

Today's throughline: *the map is not the territory, but a good map changes what you build.* We spent months building ZAOOS organically -- features, research, agents, bots, all piling into one repo. Doc 2691 is the first time we've stepped back and asked: what does this want to be? The answer is elegant. ZAOOS is the lab. The lab never ships to end users. It ships to other repos. The things that graduate become products. The things that stay become infrastructure. That distinction -- between a product and a lab -- is worth holding onto. Every project, every idea, every lane should answer: is this lab work or product work? If you know the answer, you know where it lives. And if you know where it lives, you know how to ship it.
