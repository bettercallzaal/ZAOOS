## Week's Best Idea

**The cost of a turn is the cost of looking without acting.**

The agent spend measurement showed that a turn costs about $1.01, flat regardless of session length. Which means the expensive loops aren't the ones that do a lot — they're the ones that check a lot without finding anything. A polling loop that reports "nothing changed" five times has cost $5 to tell you silence.

The insight flips the usual instinct: don't think in terms of how much you accomplish per turn. Think in terms of how many turns you spend looking versus acting. Batch the reads. Stop the loop when the queue is empty. The cost of silence is the same as the cost of work.

This connects directly to `workflow-discipline.md` rule 2 (loop governance) and `agent-spend.md`. Both now exist as behavioral rules. The loop is only worth running if there's work arriving on its own.
