## What WYDE is (verified, but press-sourced - confirm the ZAO-specific terms with WYDE)

- A decentralized impact exchange on **Base**, structured as a **Wyoming DUNA** (Decentralized Unincorporated Nonprofit Association - gives token-holder votes legal force). Mainnet launched **2025-12-10**. [press-sourced]
- **Cause coins:** each charges a dynamic **1-5% trading fee**, split (per press coverage) **25% to the verified 501c3 / 25% token holders / 25% platform ops / 25% infrastructure**. The flagship **$EAT** (Feed the Children) reportedly uses a **50% direct / 50% holder-directed** variant. [press-sourced]
- **EAT Visa debit card** (issued by i3 Bank, launched Jul 2026): a slice of everyday swipes funds the cause - the "contributory consumption" model beyond trading. Real, but a bigger lift (bank + card infra) - not something to lead with. [press-sourced]
- **Governance (2026):** holders vote on grant allocations, binding via the DUNA structure, recorded on Base. [press-sourced]

Caveat: the WYDE facts above come from press/news coverage (techbuzz.ai, causeartist, globenewswire, theblock), not from WYDE's own primary docs in this pass. Treat the mechanics as directionally correct; confirm the exact $MUSIC terms with Aaron.
