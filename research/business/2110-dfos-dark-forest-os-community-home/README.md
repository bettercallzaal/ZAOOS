# 2110 - DFOS (Dark Forest OS) - a candidate community home for ZABAL Games

> **Goal:** Assess DFOS as a home for the ZABAL Games (or ZAO) community - Zaal already made a space there - and set up the hands-on exploration (which needs his live browser).

## What it is (verified from docs.dfos.com + search)

**Dark Forest OS (DFOS), by metalabel** - "infrastructure for new private internets where groupchats, members, money, and private feeds work together in one shared operating system." Positioned against extractive social networks: *"Every group gets a private internet of its own - a place to gather, make, and share what they earn."*

- **A "space" = chat + posts + a treasury + an archive + a door you control.** Each group's space is furnished/designed independently.
- **Crypto-native identity:** an open protocol for cryptographic identity + verifiable content; identity derives from **Ed25519 signed operations, not platform accounts**. "Everything you make stays attributed to you, and you can leave with it."
- **Money is built in:** keys, a store, wallets, **treasuries**, refunds.
- **Privacy-first:** "Without AI models training on your work. Without data harvesting and extraction."
- Open reference implementations, schemas + tests at github.com/metalabel/dfos (metalabel = a credible creative-collective/releases builder).

## Why it fits ZAO / ZABAL Games

DFOS is almost a description of what ZAO wants a community home to be:
- **Member-owned + leave-with-your-content** = the ZAO ethos (100% donations, social capital, member-run) made infrastructural.
- **Treasury + wallets + store built in** = directly serves the Sparkz / creator-coin / Respect vision - a community with its own money layer, native.
- **Ed25519 signed identity** = crypto-native, matches ZAO's web3 DNA (and the "signed action" idea that also shows up in Buzz, doc 2109).
- **Private, non-extractive** = the antidote to Discord/platform lock-in; ZAO members own their space.

Zaal has **already created a ZABAL Games space** on it (`space_t8v7497vazc6tztkvvan7c`) - so this is a live evaluation, not hypothetical: is DFOS the home for the ZABAL Games community (vs Discord/Telegram)?

## The Buzz + DFOS pairing (a useful frame)

Two crypto-native, signed-identity OSes surfaced the same night, hitting ZAO's **two** coordination needs from opposite ends:
- **Buzz** (doc 2109) = the **agent/dev substrate** - humans + AI agents as teammates, git + patches + CI in-channel. For the FLEET.
- **DFOS** = the **community home** - chat + posts + treasury + archive for a creative group. For the MEMBERS.

ZAO currently spreads these across Farcaster + cowork board + Telegram + Discord. Buzz and DFOS are each a more-owned, crypto-native consolidation of one half. Worth tracking as a pair.
