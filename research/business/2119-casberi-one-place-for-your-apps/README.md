# 2119 - Casberi - "one place for your apps" (the product-answer to Zaal's fragmentation)

> **Goal:** Assess Casberi (casberi.app) - a unified home-screen aggregating dozens of services - for ZAO/Zaal, since it directly solves the app/tab/terminal fragmentation Zaal was fighting this very session.

## What it is (verified from casberi.app)

**Casberi = "One place for your apps."** A single home-screen / aggregation hub that consolidates dozens of third-party services into one interface, solving "app fragmentation fatigue" - the cost of context-switching across dozens of apps. Built by **accountless.eth** (Farcaster FID 880), a heavyweight builder: ex-Trust Wallet, Sequence, Pimlico, POAP, Polygon, MetaMask, Paxful, Flipside, Dash, Bridgewater, DARPA, DoD, Navy, DIA.

Integrations span exactly the modern creator/knowledge-worker stack:
- **Social:** Farcaster, Bluesky, Reddit, LinkedIn, Twitter, Pinterest
- **Media:** YouTube, Twitch, Apple Music, Spotify, Steam
- **Comms:** Gmail, iCloud Mail, RSS
- **Productivity/dev:** Notion, Todoist, Cal.com, Calendly, GitHub, Linear
- **AI/crypto:** ChatGPT, Claude, OpenRouter, Venice, Bankr

His casts frame it as a better home screen: *"why don't wallets show you stuff like this?"*, *"home screen"*, *"now on casberi."*

This session Zaal literally said *"I just wanna clean up my workflow"* while looking at a Chrome window with ~15 tabs and 4+ terminals. **Casberi is the consumer product that solves exactly that**, and it aggregates his actual stack: Farcaster (his social home + where he's focusing), Twitch (his streams - the 62 VODs), YouTube, Cal.com (the ZABAL Games slot booker), Notion, GitHub, Reddit, Claude, OpenRouter, Bankr. Almost every surface ZAO touches is a Casberi integration.

1. **Personal command center.** Casberi is worth trying as Zaal's own "one screen" - it targets the exact fragmentation pain. It overlaps with what the ZAO `cockpit` / `zao-harness` do (the fleet's unified view, doc 2104), but for his *consumer* apps rather than the fleet.
2. **Reference for the ZAO cockpit.** A heavyweight-built "one place for everything" is a design reference for the cockpit/harness Zaal is hand-building. Steal the good parts (per the `design-steal` skill).
3. **Farcaster-native distribution.** It's Farcaster-native, and Zaal is going Farcaster-first (doc 2106). If ZAO surfaces (the ZABAL space, WaveWarZ, the newsletter) could become Casberi integrations, that's distribution to its user base. And **accountless.eth is a builder worth knowing** - his wallet/crypto/intel resume is rare; a warm Farcaster connection is cheap.

Note the pattern with tonight's other finds: **Buzz** (doc 2109, agent/dev substrate), **DFOS** (doc 2110, community home), and now **Casberi** (personal app-aggregator) are three different "consolidate the fragmentation" bets surfacing at once - agent, community, and personal layers. ## The honest caveats

- **Early / invite-ish.** "now on casberi" reads like an early-access launch; maturity unknown. Try it, don't depend on it yet.
- **Aggregators live or die on integration depth + trust** (you're connecting Gmail, wallets, etc.). Evaluate what data it touches before wiring your accounts - same credential caution as always (don't hand it keys you wouldn't hand anyone).
- **Not a ZAO build** - it's a product to try + track, and a design reference, not something to fork.
