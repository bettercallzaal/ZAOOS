## Why it fits ZAO right now

- **Zaal is already building AI-native businesses** (Sparkz, Zoostr, WaveWarZ, ZABAL Games) - Audos funds and accelerates exactly this. The grant money could bankroll a ZAO launch that's happening regardless.
- **Zoostr is the ideal entry:** brand-new (doc 2121), AI-native, pre-revenue, needs a "0 -> paying customers" push - which is literally the Camp's arc. The unlimited Fable/Sol credits + Otto could accelerate the Zoostr build (which ZOL started).
- **The network:** operators who've built $1B+/yr companies + hundreds of builders across Asia/Europe/US = distribution + partners for ZAO surfaces.
- **Cheap-AI ladder fit:** unlimited build credits is free capacity that offsets the Claude-cap constraint for well-specced builds.

## The honest caveats

- **Time:** it's a 4-week program with weekly actions. Zaal's plate is full - treat it as "enter Zoostr, use the credits + grant, do the minimum weekly action," not a fifth full-time thread. The "one clear action per week" framing is designed for exactly this.

- **Grants likely have strings** (build-on-Audos, spend inside their ecosystem) - confirm what the $500/$5K can actually be spent on before over-counting it.
