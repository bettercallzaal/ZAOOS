ZABAL Games - Final Month

August is comprised of the July submitters.

If you submitted anything in July - a track, a build, a draft, a work in progress - you are already in the August Finals. No application. No gate. July was the open door; August is what you do with it.

See the finals pool - the submissions board

How August works

Weeks 1-2: Open Qualifier (Aug 3-16)

  • Every July submitter is on the leaderboard.
  • Post a daily project update to earn points.
  • The Farcaster community votes on projects.
  • Half your score is your daily updates, half is the community.
  • Top 2 per track advance.

Weeks 3-4: The Battles (Aug 17-30)

  • Top 2 per track go head-to-head, one round per week.
  • Finalists build and present on stream.
  • Settlement is multi-factor - community, mentors, and WaveWarZ signals combined. No single judge.
  • One ZABAL Games winner crowned end of August.

The rules, in plain words

  • Daily updates are public - build in public IS the qualifier.
  • Community votes: one vote per Farcaster account per project per week.
  • Mentors pair with builders through the month and share the evaluation criteria openly.
  • Partial work counts. Showing up daily beats polishing silently.

ZABAL Games is The ZAO's 3-month build-a-thon: June workshops, July open build, August finals. Free to join, built for a real community of 100+ members. Find us on Farcaster in /zabal.
