## Zaal's idea (2026-07-31, verbatim intent)

Each of the two weeks is a challenge. **Week 1: share your submission with the community, and see what you can improve from the beginning of Week 1 to the end of Week 1, from one of your submissions. The most improved is what we are looking for - especially if you share who helped you and what they supported with.**

So Week 1 rewards **delta, not polish**: pick a July submission, put it in front of the community, level it up over the week, and the biggest jump wins - with explicit credit to your collaborators.

## Why this is a strong, differentiated format

Standard hackathon judging scores the FINISHED artifact (technical execution, impact/feasibility, UX, and - less commonly - teamwork). A survey of the common frameworks (EventFlare, Mettl, TAIKAI, DoraHacks, MLH) turned up impact / execution / creativity / presentation as the near-universal axes - and **no standard "Most Improved" or collaboration-credit category.** That absence is the point: ZABAL's Week 1 is doing something the hackathon playbook does not, and it fits The ZAO's DNA:

- **It rewards trajectory, so a rough July submission is an asset, not a liability** - the person with the most room to grow can win. That is the most inclusive possible on-ramp and retroactively makes every July submission valuable.
- **It makes collaboration the mechanic, not a footnote.** "Share who helped you and what they supported with" turns mutual aid into a scored, visible behavior - which is exactly Respect / contribution culture ([[project_fractal_process]]) expressed as a challenge.
- **It is anti-polish, pro-progress** - aligned with July's "partial and WIP counts" ethos.

## How to run + judge it (operational)

**The delta problem:** "most improved" needs a defensible before/after. Make the start-state explicit so the improvement is auditable:

1. **Start-of-week snapshot.** At Week 1 open, each participant names the ONE July submission they are improving and links its current state (repo/URL/video/track as it stands). That is the baseline. (On Loops House this is a submission field: "Which July submission are you improving?" + "Start-of-week state (link).")
2. **The week.** Share it with the community (the /zabal channel + Spaces). Get help. Iterate.
3. **End-of-week submission.** Link the improved version + a short "what changed" + the required **"Who helped you, and what did they support with?"** (already added to the Loops House form 2026-07-31). Optionally: a 1-line self-assessment of the biggest jump.

**Judging signal = the size + honesty of the jump, weighted by credited collaboration.** Suggested rubric (keep it light, this is Week 1 not the finale):
- **Improvement delta (50%)** - how much better, start vs end. The core.
- **Collaboration (30%)** - did they pull in help AND credit it specifically? Named, specific credit scores higher than "thanks everyone." This is the behavior being incentivized.
- **Shared in public (20%)** - did they actually put it in front of the community during the week (not a private grind)?

The ZAO (the sponsor/judge on Loops House) applies this. Because it is trajectory-based, it does not need the WaveWarZ market - Week 1 is judged, the FINALE is the WaveWarZ battles (doc 2137 / the canonical copy).

## Loops House config implied by this (feeds the "update everything" step)

- **Submission fields for Week 1:** "Which July submission are you improving?" (link, start state), "What did you improve?" (text), "Who helped you, and what did they support with?" (text, required - DONE), plus the improved-version link. The generic Description/Repo/Demo fields cover the rest.
- **About copy:** the About currently says the generic "weekly tasks" line. When Zaal locks it, update it to name Week 1 = Most Improved explicitly (share -> improve -> credit helpers -> biggest jump wins).
- **Judging:** The ZAO (sponsor) scores Week 1 by the rubric above; Loops House's sponsor-picks flow publishes the Week 1 result.
