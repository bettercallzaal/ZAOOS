## The constraint the Week 2 challenge has to satisfy

Week 2 sits between Week 1 (Most Improved: take a July submission, improve it with community help, credit your helpers) and the **Finale** (top 2 per track -> 3-5 WaveWarZ battles -> one winner per track). So Week 2 must:
- **Progress the arc** - not repeat Week 1's "improve your existing thing."
- **Produce something battle-ready** - the finale is WaveWarZ battles, so by end of Week 2 each track needs its top 2 to have something worth trading on.
- **Stay on-brand** - collaboration-first, contribution-over-polish, music-first, partial-counts.
- Reuse the same Loops House submission mechanics (title / pitch / description / link + the "who helped" field).

## Three options (with a recommendation)

### Option A - "Ship New" (progression: improve -> create)
Week 2 = a fresh weekly task per track: build something NEW (not last week's thing). Week 1 rewarded trajectory on an old idea; Week 2 rewards a clean-slate build under a prompt. By end of Week 2 each participant has an improved-thing (Wk1) + a new-thing (Wk2); the finale picks the top 2 per track from the stronger of the two.
- **Pro:** simple, familiar hackathon shape, clear deliverable.
- **Con:** doesn't deepen the collaboration theme; two disconnected builds.

### Option B - "Pay It Forward" (RECOMMENDED - doubles down on the ZAO thesis)
Week 2 = **become the helper.** The task is to meaningfully support other participants' builds - and you are scored on the value you added to OTHERS (verified by their "who helped you" credits from Week 1 + Week 2). Week 1 asked "who helped you?"; Week 2 makes helping the game. Your own build still progresses, but the standout Week-2 signal is **how many people credited YOU and for what.**
- **Pro:** this is Respect/contribution culture ([[project_fractal_process]]) turned into a challenge - the most ZAO thing possible, and nothing standard-hackathon does it. It compounds the Week 1 collaboration flywheel (helpers get rewarded, so more people help). It also naturally surfaces who the community MVPs are heading into the finale.
- **Con:** needs a clean way to attribute + verify help (the "who helped you" field is the ledger; a helper's score = the sum of credits they received). More novel = more to explain.

### Option C - "Battle Prep" (progression: build -> make it tradeable)
Week 2 = get finale-ready. Take your strongest build and make it **WaveWarZ-battle-ready**: a tight pitch, a demo, the thing framed so the community can judge/trade it in the finale. The Week 2 deliverable IS the finale entry.
- **Pro:** the most direct on-ramp to the WaveWarZ battles; no wasted motion; the finale isn't a cold start.
- **Con:** thinner as a standalone "challenge" - more prep than build.

## Recommendation

**Option B ("Pay It Forward"), with a light dose of C.** Week 1 = improve your thing with help; Week 2 = flip it, you help others and get credited; then the top 2 per track (by combined signal) go into the WaveWarZ finale. This makes the whole two weeks a **collaboration engine** - the exact opposite of a solo leaderboard grind, and the truest expression of The ZAO's contribution ethos. Fold in a Battle-Prep nudge at the end of Week 2 so finalists arrive at the WaveWarZ battles with something tradeable.

If Zaal wants simpler, **Option A** is the safe default and easiest to run/judge.

## What each option implies for Loops House + judging

| Option | Loops House field(s) to add | Judging signal |
|--------|-----------------------------|----------------|
| A Ship New | reuse the standard fields for a new build | quality/impact of the new build |
| B Pay It Forward | "Who did you help this week, and how?" (mirror of Wk1's field) | count + substance of credits you RECEIVED |
| C Battle Prep | "Your finale pitch (one line)" + demo link | battle-readiness / tradeability |

(The Week 1 "Who helped you" field already added to the live Loops House form 2026-07-31 doubles as the attribution ledger Option B needs.)
