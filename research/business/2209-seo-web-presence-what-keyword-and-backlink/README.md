## Findings

**The topical keyword "web3 music community" has no incumbent.** SimilarWeb's top-keyword data, fetched directly on 2026-08-06, shows that all three sites - thezao.com, Sound.xyz, and Royal.io - rank only for brand-navigational queries: "zao," "jadyn violet," "sound xyz vault," "royal music." None appears in the public keyword data for any informational term including "web3 music community," "music NFT platform," or "how to sell music on blockchain." This is the most actionable finding: the head keyword thezao.com wants to own has no current winner.

**thezao.com is outranked but the gap is smaller than it appears.** SimilarWeb global rank for thezao.com is #8,167,314 versus Sound.xyz at #5,743,034 and Royal.io at #3,573,169. But thezao.com is the only one growing (+66.49% month-over-month versus Sound.xyz -61.17% and Royal.io -25.32%). The trajectory favors The ZAO; the authority and content volume still do not.

**The backlink authority gap is real and measurable.** Sound.xyz raised $5M from a16z in 2021, generating TechCrunch coverage (fetched via WebSearch, 2026-08-06) - a DA 90+ backlink. Royal.io has a Wikipedia article (fetched via WebSearch - first organic result). thezao.com has no equivalent press placement visible in public data. Domain authority is not directly readable from SimilarWeb's public view, but the presence of Wikipedia and tier-1 tech press coverage for competitors versus nothing for thezao.com represents a structural authority deficit. Closing it requires one earned editorial placement, not a link-building campaign.

**All three platforms are editorially thin on informational content.** Sound.xyz publishes artist spotlights on Substack ("This artist is the master of building community in web3," soundxyz.substack.com - indexed via WebSearch, 2026-08-06). This off-site editorial presence is not reflected as a ranking keyword. Royal.io has no visible editorial program. thezao.com has no indexed editorial content in the SimilarWeb keyword set. The opportunity is informational content that earns topical authority for "web3 music community" - currently zero competition from either rival.

**Community discussion about these platforms is absent on HN.** An Algolia API query to hn.algolia.com for "sound xyz web3 music community" returned 0 hits (fetched 2026-08-06, FULL). Reddit search returned a 403 block (FAILED). This absence is itself a finding: none of the three platforms has built meaningful developer or tech community credibility, which is a long-tail distribution channel that also drives backlinks. HN and developer-adjacent communities are uncontested territory for whoever publishes the first credible "how web3 music economics work" technical explainer.

**Sound.xyz's declining audience is a warm acquisition target for comparison content.** Sound.xyz traffic dropped 61% in June 2026. Its top similar site is earmilk.com (a music discovery blog), suggesting its audience is music-first and actively seeking alternatives. A comparison page - "Sound.xyz vs Royal vs The ZAO: which web3 music community is right for you?" - would intercept this migration intent. Royal.io's top competitors on SimilarWeb are NFT marketplace adjacents (ton.diamonds, minted.network), a different audience segment.
