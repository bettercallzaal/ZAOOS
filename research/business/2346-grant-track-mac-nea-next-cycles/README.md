# 2346 — Grant track: the next MAC cycle, and why the NEA thread is not what doc 1372 thinks

> **Why this doc:** board card 64beb12c asks for the next Maine Arts Commission cycle dates and the NEA
> next step, so the next deadline is a tap rather than a scramble. Doc 1372 is the ground. Two of its
> load-bearing assumptions are wrong, and the correction changes what we should do for the next ~2 years.

## TL;DR

1. **Fractured Atlas does not unlock the NEA. It disqualifies us.** NEA Grants for Arts Projects states
   in terms that funding is *not* available for "applications submitted by a fiscal sponsor". Doc 1372
   lists NEA as "BLOCKED by FA / FA fiscal sponsorship required" and says FA "also unlocks NEA". That is
   backwards.
2. **Even with a 501(c)(3), NEA is 2-4 years away.** GAP requires 5 completed years of arts programming
   and $20,000+ in operating expenses in the last completed fiscal year, plus a 1:1 cost share.
3. **The one MAC opportunity we can actually enter is the Maine Artist Fellowship: opens Sept 1, closes
   Sept 30, 2026. $5,000, no match, no 501(c)(3) required.** But it is a career-merit award for an
   individual artist, not project money for ZAOstock.
4. **The MAC Organization Operations Grant (Sept 15 - Oct 15) is double-blocked** - it needs 501(c)(3)
   status *and* an annual operating budget above $25,000. We have neither.
5. There is an **unresolved contradiction on the board** about whether Fisher was submitted. Parked for
   Zaal - see "One thing only Zaal can settle".

## Correction 1: the NEA does not accept fiscal-sponsored applications

Verbatim from `arts.gov/grants/grants-for-arts-projects`, fetched 2026-08-20:

> "Funding is **not** available in this category for individuals, **applications submitted by a fiscal
> sponsor**, commercial/for-profit enterprises, or State Arts Agencies and Regional Arts Organizations."

Eligible applicants are, verbatim: "Nonprofit, tax-exempt 501(c)(3), U.S. organizations; Units of state
or local government; or Federally recognized tribal communities or tribes."

Doc 1372's hard constraint reads: *"Fisher Grant requires Fractured Atlas fiscal sponsorship FIRST... FA
also unlocks NEA and other grants."* The Fisher half may well be right. **The NEA half is not.** A
Fractured Atlas-sponsored application to GAP would be marked ineligible.

This does not make Fractured Atlas worthless - it still enables tax-deductible donations and it is
still the stated route for Fisher and for MAC's organizational track. It just is **not** a path to the
NEA, and doc 1372's Grant 6 row should be corrected rather than waited on.

### The other three NEA gates, all verbatim

- "Applicant organizations must have completed **at least 5 years of arts programming** prior to the
  application deadline."
- "An applicant organization must have had a minimum of **$20,000 in organization operating expenses**
  in its most recently completed fiscal year."
- "**1:1 Cost share required.** Sources may include both cash and in-kind."
- "Generally, an applicant may submit one application per calendar year."

**Challenge America** (the small NEA track, `arts.gov/grants/challenge-america`) requires an operating
budget **under $250,000** and a request of **exactly $10,000** - the budget ceiling is the one NEA rule
we comfortably clear, but it sits inside the same GAP eligibility frame above.

### NEA dates, as published

Both FY2026 GAP cycles have closed:

| Milestone | Cycle 1 | Cycle 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Part 1 (Grants.gov) deadline | Feb 12, 2026 | **Jul 9, 2026** |
| Part 2 portal opens | Feb 18, 2026 | Jul 14, 2026 |
| Part 2 deadline | Feb 25, 2026 | **Jul 21, 2026** |
| Notification | Nov 2026 | Apr 2027 |
| Earliest project start | Jan 1, 2027 | Jun 1, 2027 |

**No FY2027 dates are published on that page yet.** By the pattern above, expect Part 1 around
early-to-mid February 2027. Watch the page; do not diarise a guessed date.

**Recommendation: stop treating NEA as a live thread.** Move it to a watch item gated on the ZAO having
its own 501(c)(3) and five years of documented programming. Revisit when both are true, not before.

## Correction 2: what MAC actually has open

Source: `mainearts.maine.gov/Pages/Funding/Grants-Home`, fetched 2026-08-20. MAC's own note: *"Dates are
subject to change."*

### The two with published upcoming dates

| Program | Opens | Deadline | Max | Match | Who |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Maine Artist Fellowship** | **Sept 1, 2026** | **Sept 30, 2026** | $5,000 | None | Individual artists |
| Organization Operations Grant | Sept 15, 2026 | Oct 15, 2026 | $7,500 | 1-to-1 | 501(c)(3) orgs |

Award notifications: mid-January 2027 and January 2027 respectively.

**The Organization Operations Grant is not available to us.** Verbatim: it "provides operating support
to **501(c)(3) arts organizations** in Maine with **annual operating budgets above $25,000**." The ZAO
has neither. Note this is a *stricter* gate than fiscal sponsorship solves - even with Fractured Atlas,
the budget floor stands. Do not spend effort here.

**The Maine Artist Fellowship is enterable now**, and this is the actionable finding of this doc. But be
clear about what it is, verbatim from MAC: *"Fellowships are not grants for artist projects. They are
merit-based awards evaluated by a review panel on the level of artistic creativity and vision shown in
the artists' support materials."* One artist is selected per discipline across six disciplines - Fine
Crafts, Literary Arts, Media Arts, Performing Arts, Traditional Arts, Visual Arts.

So it funds **Zaal as an artist**, on the strength of a body of work, and it cannot be pitched as
ZAOstock funding. Media Arts or Performing Arts are the plausible disciplines.

### The critical eligibility line

Verbatim, from the ARTIST FUNDING section: *"Applicants **do not need to be affiliated with a 501(c)(3)
organization** in order to be eligible for funding."*

**This confirms doc 1478's note and it is the most useful sentence on the page.** The entire individual-
artist track at MAC is outside the Fractured Atlas gate. Everything blocked on FA is blocked only on the
*organizational* side.

### Closed, but worth a calendar watch

| Program | Max | Match | 501(c)(3)? | Last awarded |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Artist Project Grant** | $2,500 | None | **No** | FY 2027 |
| Creative Communities Grant | $7,000 | 1-to-1 | Org track | FY 2027 |
| Craft Apprenticeship Program | $4,500 | None | No | 2026 |
| Traditional Arts Apprenticeship | $3,000 | None | via Cultural Resources | - |

**The Artist Project Grant is the one to want.** $2,500, no match, no 501(c)(3) needed, and it funds
"the creation of new or existing work... and supporting ways to bring their art to the public" - which
is a far better fit for a ZAOstock-shaped ask than the Fellowship is. It is currently **Closed** with no
announced reopening. MAC says guidelines are updated annually and grants support work occurring between
July 1 and June 30, so a reopening in the FY2027-28 planning window is plausible but **unannounced -
do not diarise a guessed date**. Watch the Grants Home page and the MAC e-news.
