## The Sparkz tokenomics conversation (the important part)

**The mechanism Zaal is excited about (Clanker v5):** fee recipients are changeable after the fact. So you create split wallets that are all of your tokens as a creator, and every time you mint a new token, it (a) splits its fee earnings back to the main wallet, and (b) the main wallet adds that new token as a fee recipient too. Result: **every token in your ecosystem benefits from a buy on any of them** - synchronicity, not dilution. A sell on one can even trigger a small buy on each of the others. This is the opposite of the normal "every new token dilutes the last."

**James's framing - "money as the membrane":** Sparks is the core community token / money membrane. Communities and tokens pair to it and ride on top as ecosystem tokens; how the community interacts affects the price on the financial markets. James: "Sparks would be your core community token, and then we build a factory on top of that that's paired with Sparks."

**The token factory:** James offers to build a **permissioned, cookie-cutter token factory** on top of Sparks - launch a token with a button press, but keyed so **not anyone** can do it (community votes to add a new token / member), avoiding the open-public shitcoin flood. A standard doorway pool (ETH or stablecoin) is the entry; the Sparks token is the doorway; other tokens ride on top as ecosystem tokens.

**Fee-share / yield tracking (already a solved primitive):** James's "bananas" on X are yield-tracking tokens he just hasn't activated. Mechanism: mint a fee-share token; whoever holds it gets a share of the fees, split equally across the fee shares; mint and distribute them to change the balance of where fees flow. James: "that is essentially what it sounds like you're going to want to do" - i.e. Sparks already has a proven primitive to build on.

**Zaal's intent for the first Sparks token:** take ~90% out at launch (a deliberate whale-scarer) with the intent of it flowing back to future creations. Pay contributors in Sparks for creating PRs / building on the repo (bounty-board style), eventually tied to POIDH. Requirements to "spark up" a token: an **open-source GitHub repo**, a **visible/aligned founder identity**, and **ZAO Respect** used for social capital + curation.

**Endowment tooling (James's other offer):** James builds endowment tooling (currently education-focused, wants an art endowment). Proposal: art-endowment tokens whose **charity leg funnels to the ZAO** or to a specific ZAO project (e.g. the festivals), with yield streams baked into each project's token as it grows, baked into the token factory. Prompted by "do you want this to outlive you?" -> the ZAO bus test (Samantha + AttaBotty hold two halves of the ZAO wallets).
