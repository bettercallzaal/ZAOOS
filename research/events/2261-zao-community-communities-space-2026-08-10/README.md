## The ZAO Festivals lineage, first time recorded in one place

Zaal walked the whole history. This is the clearest statement of it we have:

| # | Event | What Zaal said |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **ZAO Palooza** (New York) | Six weeks of planning, six people helping, **zero dollars out of pocket from the community**, twelve performances - six from non-Web3 individuals and six from on-chain musicians |
| 2 | **ZAOchella** (Miami) | Six months of planning. *"a phenomenal opportunity to get the ZAO brand and name out there"* |
| 3 | **2025** | Focus shifted to **WaveWarZ** as a brand rather than a festival |
| 4 | **ZAOville** (2026, weeks ago) | **DeCoup's third annual VEC event, at his own house and venue.** The ZAO came in and added attention, promotion and support rather than running it |

The founding thesis, verbatim: *"everyone kept talking about all these side events at crypto conferences... and then no one coming to these events during bear markets. I thought, us being a music community, if we did events with musicians, people will always show up for the musicians whether or not the price of XYZ crypto is at XYZ amount."*

And on why the festivals matter to the builders: *"it's been really cool to see all of the musicians become builders... they realized the platforms weren't there to help support them, so they had to build their own platform - not to capture value from someone else, but to solve a simple problem that they were facing themselves."*

## Eight things that are NEW for ZAOstock

**1. Lion's Den is locked in.** Confirmed during the Space: *"we got Lion's Den locked in in this last week."* Found via DeCoup - he performed at ZAOville, and his track "Hands Up High" opened this Space.

**2. Flenz is confirmed to perform.** *"one big musician... that has been actually a part of the whole planning alongside and kind of helping support me on the organizational hand and is going to be coming down to perform is going to be Flenz."* He is both a performer and an organiser here.

**3. P2P / Bomb Squad are coming to shoot ZAOstock.** They captured ZAOville end to end with disposable cameras, drones and multiple cameras, and built the gallery. Paper Hand's reasoning is worth keeping: *"there is always a lack in the historical documentation... helping the artists bring their own brand to the platform."*

**4. WaveWarZ will run live at ZAOstock, in a defined shape.** *"at a very minimum, the goal is to have four musicians do three different battles - one in the middle of the day, the second three-fourths of the way through, and then the last one being that finals."* Promoted by time slot so the online audience can join, streamed live to bring the international community in.

**5. A fire permit is needed.** DeCoup fire-spins with a headset mic, and Zaal wants it at ZAOstock: *"we have to find a place in Ellsworth that will let us fire spin."* DeCoup has pulled fire permits before and offered to check.

**6. Heart of Ellsworth made a 2.5-minute org video** about the town. Zaal has supported that nonprofit for roughly eight to ten months and wants to use it to introduce Ellsworth to the online community.

**7. The venue's geography is a selling point we are not using.** Thirty minutes from **Acadia National Park**, and early October is peak fall colour in Maine. Zaal pitched it live as a reason to travel.

**8. Zaal said the site is behind.** Verbatim: *"I've been a little bit lacking on some of the ZAOstock updates... hopefully update the website and stuff."* This doc is the input for that.

## Also new, outside ZAOstock

- **Decentraland.** The "biggest Web3 metaverse" teased early was named at the close: COC and The ZAO are hosting a joint Space with **Decentraland and the Decentraland community next week**, on X. The link was to be posted immediately after this Space.
- **WaveWarZ runs 11 shows a week** - Zaal's own figure.
- **DeCoup's "Supernova Light" is already in the WaveWarZ catalog.** He battled Louie and lost the first two rounds by saving his strongest track for the end - a strategy note worth telling new artists, since the format rewards leading with strength.
- **Quan proposed a "Pokemon Showdown all-randoms" format** for WaveWarZ - random songs drawn from the top catalog, forcing players to fight with what they are given.
- **The play-out track** was "Yo Candy (Where the models at)" by **Benny featuring Stilo**, built from phrases Stilo uses in the WaveWarZ space.
- **Miss Evie hosts a Tuesday Metal House space**, 9pm UK.
