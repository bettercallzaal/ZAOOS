## Local intel worth keeping

- **Discover Ellsworth** - the right plug for visitors deciding where to go. Heart of Ellsworth covers downtown identity; Discover Ellsworth covers the visit itself.
- **Lodging** - the High Street district is where the hotels are; there is no downtown Ellsworth hotel yet. Arbor Camp is doing lodging packages for a November event and is worth approaching.
- **Green space and walkability** - Norton Park, the Franklin Street Parklet, the Ellsworth Riverwalk behind the public library, Harbor Park, and the Ellsworth Farmers Market on Saturday mornings. Frenchman Bay Conservancy runs local trails.
- **Heart of Ellsworth** has been a Main Street America accredited community since 2024, now in its second year.
- **Spectrum Reach** is running a regional video and digital asset campaign for Art of Ellsworth.
- **Fogtoberfest** at Fogtown Brewing falls on the same weekend - a conflict and an opportunity.
