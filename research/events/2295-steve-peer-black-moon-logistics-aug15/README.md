## The headline

Steve Peer IS the Black Moon connection - and Katina (Black Moon) is the
gift-card decision-maker. The Bendigo transport contact originated as Steve's
referral on this call ("He's a great guy... runs a van to Boston").

## Decisions (all explicit on the call)

1. **Two stages.** Outside + inside at Black Moon, sets alternating so the
   music never stops - "there's no gap in the music." Roughly half the acts
   inside, half outside.
2. **Steve MCs part of the event.** "I'd be glad to... do whatever copy you
   come up with and improv."
3. **Bar percentage deal:** a minimum revenue threshold activates a percentage
   share; either side drafts the contract. Zaal offered to write it.
4. **After party:** 12-6 is the official two-stage show; 6pm+ moves inside,
   casual, lighter setup.
5. **Performer feeding via gift cards:** Katina willing to issue Black Moon
   gift cards per performer; the same concept extends to 86 This (wraps),
   Sailor, and Flexit - three meals covered without formal catering.
6. **Boston artist routing:** time flights to land together in Boston (cheaper
   than Bangor/Portland), van up to Ellsworth - Bendigo, possibly meeting
   Tuesday. Steve has Boston-connection backups and notes a good bus service.

## Facts for the record

- 6 out-of-state artists CONFIRMED flying out + 2 local Maine acts; ~2/3 of
  artist travel already funded by Zaal; he will self-fund the rest if needed.
- Under 20-25 performers total. Sets are 20 or 40 minutes.
- Zaal will work from Black Moon several days the week of Aug 17; in-person
  logistics walk Tuesday (cameras, drone photos). Black Moon may close Monday.
- Steve + Katina invited to the Monday 11:30 private team call and Tuesday
  11:30 public space.
- iPad stations inside showing the stream; goal is measurable local-business
  lift ("this is the amount additional we added in a day").
- Housing: hotel block, Friday + Saturday nights, group rate, one floor.
- Zaal's fiancee went to school with the Flexit contact - the catering warm lead.
