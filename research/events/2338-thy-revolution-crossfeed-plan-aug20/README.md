## Thread 1 - The 30-second cross-feed format

### The Idea

Thy Revolution's pitch: for longer-form content, each side gives the 30-second
breakdown of what they took from the other's piece, then points at the full
video. Zaal extends it into a recording habit - at the end of every long call,
before pressing end, say "if you want to know more the full video is right
here", then clip that segment and use it as the sign-off. Thy adds the intro
side: "here are the three things from the last video that people found
impactful", and a personal "here's one thing that resonated with me right now
because of a, b and c."

### Why It Matters

It makes each recording yield its own promo without a separate shoot, and gives
both brands a reason to surface the other's content. Zaal: "that's exactly what
I need."

### Next Step

Zaal adopts the sign-off habit (row 5). Zaal picks a posting day per platform
(row 4).

## Thread 2 - Thy Revolution's platform plan

Thy's "semi plan that's going to be refined": double down on X; post the same
content to TikTok simultaneously; soon start a weekly 30-60 minute TikTok live;
then move onto the other socials as teams - go to LinkedIn together, "people see
that there's a lot of life where we are", and bring people back to X. The party
analogy: you do not tell people there is a party down the street, "you are the
party" - you bring your party to theirs and bring them back.

Zaal's parallel: TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn - pick one day a week for each and
attack. Zaal and Thy also referenced the Decentraland Space as "an amazing
space" (06:13-06:19) - that is the COC x ZAO x Decentraland X Space announced in
[doc 2261](../2261-zao-community-communities-space-2026-08-10/).
