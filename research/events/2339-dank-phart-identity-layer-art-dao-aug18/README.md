## Thread 1 - Zaal's walk-through of the ZAO identity layer

Most of the first eight minutes is Zaal explaining the model; Dank Phart's
track is mostly short affirmations. The pieces, in Zaal's framing:

- **Blockchain agnostic, provenance first.** Storage and IP provenance can be
  handled by people with "30 years of experience"; Zaal wants to hand off
  everything that is not his specialty ("I can't be publishing ... I want to
  make sure I specialize and create").
- **Individuals, not companies.** Solo entrepreneurs and artists join under the
  ZAO tag, build an artist reputation / ZAO identity by gaining governance, and
  that identity travels on-chain - for DAO votes, time-boxed or cumulative
  weighting, or "just as an airdrop list".
- **Respect as social capital for incubation.** A member uses ZAO Respect to
  back someone else's idea - "I'm behind this idea and I endorse this idea" -
  the way DAO proposals allocate financial capital. An idea that is "Zaoified"
  (carries the Z brand) or aligned with the artist-profit / IP-rights mission
  can be a ZAO sub-DAO idea.
- **Peer-to-peer marketplace with an eBay-style seller score.** "I know what a
  seller's score is like when I ask them to be on my team for a startup."
  Dank Phart: "you're also getting a marketplace because if your people are
  coming to me being like I gotta have these things but I also need a musician
  ... or a videographer."
- **Provenance from event attendance.** "The thing that I'm building is just
  provenance using my people that are coming to my event. That's literally it."
- **Give away control so it cannot be sold badly.** "I don't want to ever be
  able to be in a bad headspace and sell it to the wrong person ... giving away
  control and governance and power to the community at large."
- **Open source and forkable**, an affiliate program "done right in a
  peer-to-peer sense", and a fraternity-style big-brother/little-brother mentor
  model - possibly invite-by-a-mentor, possibly a non-paid "pro version", both
  explicitly undecided ("but I digress").
- **Fractal is upstream.** "They've been working on it for four years and I'm
  a fourth of that"; three really active people plus accessory people; others
  have started their own things off fractals; Zaal helps formulate rather than
  own the technology. Votes are traceable, double-voting is expected not to
  happen, and Respect is "soulbound to liquid" so a lost wallet can be restored
  by community consensus.

Dank Phart's response to the mentor idea adds the one concrete extension: the
longer-tenured member also connects newcomers to the right people - "as it
grows, it's going to become an amazing network ... people that have been there
longer are going to know who's there and who to point to."

## Thread 2 - Dank Phart's art-DAO history and what they are building

Dank Phart has wanted an art DAO "for a long time". Their San Francisco
connection (the Forbes Island reference they had already sent Zaal) was where
they first tried, around 2022 - "it just spectacularly didn't ... it didn't go
anywhere. But it was like the initial ideations of this." A friend there is
"way connected to a bunch of people that are deep in Bitcoin". Their frame:
"it's all about artist sovereignty, and how we can build the means to build a
viable, sustainable career without having to sacrifice yourself in all the ways
that we are forced to."

On what they are building now, the call gives only: "we're just in building,
there's nothing really on board at the moment. And I think once Peers is ready,
I would love to figure out what building ZAO and Peers would look like." Zaal's
answer is that the ZAO model can be replicated on any chain as a smart contract,
so the bridge is not urgent: "we'll figure out when that bridge comes."

Who Greg is, what Peers is, and what Ever Archive is were not explained on the
call. A Greg exists in ZAO memory - the lawyer at Autonomous from the 2026-07-01
WaveWarZ legal call ([doc 951](../951-greg-autonomous-wavewarz-legal/))
- but nothing on this call says it is the same person, and this doc does not
assume it.
