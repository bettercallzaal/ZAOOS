## Findings

### The numbers, finally stated out loud

**Five artists are 100% confirmed.** Most of the WaveWarZ field is unconfirmed, plus
about three more outside WaveWarZ still being worked against budget. *This is the
first time the count has been said plainly in a call, and it corrects a figure that
had moved three times in four days.*

**Fundraising:** $1.6k raised from Artizen last season, which covered getting a
majority of artists there, plus a **$3,000 prize** won from an Artizen pool. Both are
time-delayed and will not arrive before Oct 3. **Zaal has committed to fronting a
portion of those funds himself** against money he knows is coming - explicitly framed
as trading the time value of money, and made easier by the certainty that it has
already been raised.

**No official paying sponsors yet.** A couple of warm local leads. The pitch deck is
the unlock.

### What the money is actually for

In priority order as stated: getting the **last couple of artists** there and
confirmed, then **housing**, then AV and decorations. Food is partly solved - Black
Moon offered roughly **$25 per person for about 25 people** in gift cards, and
suggested asking other local restaurants to match it, which doubles as a way to make
more local businesses partners.

### Arbor Camp, the artist house

A treehouse property Zaal wants as the artist house and weekend gathering point,
explicitly preferred over a block of hotel rooms. **The eight-person house is $1,000
a night.** Hotel blocks are being set up as the backup regardless. Someone at the
Heart of Ellsworth meeting has a contact, so Zaal is going through them rather than
cold-DMing. The pitch to Arbor Camp is partnership and content: a new build, a new
offering, and a house full of people making material they can use.

### The audio-interface answer

Zaal asked how to get stage audio into the stream. **Fellenz answered it directly:**
an audio interface into a computer, fed from the **main outs of the board on site**.
And the shortcut - *"ideally the soundboard, if it actually is a USB interface. A lot
of soundboards are integrated already, so you just plug that straight into the
computer."* Zaal added that he is not against buying the gear outright for reuse
across future events.

### The push server is demo-ready

Paper reported it working: a **magic link** that grants access to a control room,
from which the ticket can be customised per event. Next step is **iOS and Android
wallet APIs**, so a ticket saves to the phone wallet alongside real cards rather than
a crypto wallet - functioning like a POAP but in the wallet people actually use. It
runs on **WebRTC**.

This is what prompted decision 8. Zaal's reasoning, verbatim in spirit: free events
capture the last-minute people that paid events lose, so keep it free - but a $1
donation buying a real digital ticket is different from an RSVP, and *"we can
actually get a lot more $1 donation from 500 people than an easy $500."*

### Finder's fee - PROPOSAL, not a decision

Explicitly unfinalised on the call. As floated:

- **10%** for anyone outside the team who introduces a sponsor that converts
- **15%** for anyone on the team
- **up to 25%** if they act as the point of contact and carry the coordination

Purpose stated: give people helping organise virtually *"some skin in the game"* so
everyone can participate in being on the team. **Zaal: "None of that's formalized or
finalized."** To be decided this week.

### The deck plan, with dates

Raw deck content from Zaal to **Candy and Paper by Tuesday night**, design worked on
**Wednesday**, initial draft shared with the team after. Zaal is writing the words and
content himself and explicitly not the design - *"that's not my wheelhouse and it'll
look bad if I'm the person in charge for that."* Two versions: one for sponsors, one
to share and promote.

The unidentified participant offered a shortcut worth taking: lead with ZAO-CHELLA,
pull footage and stills from it and ZAO-PALOOZA, and spend 90 minutes to two hours
rather than building from scratch. They offered their own sponsor-winning decks as
templates.

### Logos landed during the call

Six partner logos are now in the Drive folder: **Black Moon** (2 versions), **Star
97.7** (2), **Bomb Squad** (2), **Wallace Events** (2), **WaveWarZ**, and **COC
Concertz**. Three still outstanding: **Town of Ellsworth**, **ENTERACT**,
**Web3Metal**.

**COC Concertz is to be added to the partner list** - its logo arrived but it was not
on the site's partner array.

Fellenz also dropped three image files into his own artist folder within hours of the
folders being created, which is the first evidence the structure is being used.
