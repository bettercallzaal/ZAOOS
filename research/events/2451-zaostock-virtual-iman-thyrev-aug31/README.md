## What did not happen

- **Rev's audio question went unanswered.** Whether a community Space carries the event audio or is purely a funnel is the single biggest unresolved item, because it determines what every Space host has to configure. It was asked once and the conversation moved past it.
- **No moderator names.** "Two per hour" is a decision without a single name attached to a single hour.
- **No virtual home base named.** Rev asked where people get fed from and to; the answer is implied to be the metaverse but was never stated.
- **Iman's delegation question was answered only partially.** He asked who is in charge of what in the core team beyond himself and Rev. The reply covered that a lot of people will need coordinating and that group chats will be split by purpose, but no map of who owns what was given.

## Contradictions with what we already believe

- **The organizing doc's Team and Roles tab says "Virtual - no lead."** After this call that is still literally true - no lead was appointed - but it is now misleading, because two people have a defined job and a standing sync. The tab should say so.
- **The doc treats the stream as one destination.** This call describes many simultaneous surfaces: a continuous metaverse stream plus several community-run X Spaces. That is a different shape and a different failure mode - it needs several people to have working setups, not one.
- **The AV tab's top risk is still the untested rtmps destination**, with Aziz owing the ingest URL and stream key. Nothing on this call changed that, and everything decided here sits downstream of it. Multiple Spaces pointing at a stream that has never been tested end to end is more exposure, not less.
