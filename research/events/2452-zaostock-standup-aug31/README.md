## Corrections - three things the doc had wrong

### 1. The City has ALREADY approved the fire performance

> "We have confirmed with the city that the fire spinning is going to be good to go. We just need a fire circle."

The doc and the insurance email both said we were still "working through the City's requirements". We are not - it is approved, and the outstanding item is a **fire circle**, which is equipment, not permission. **The insurance broker was told the weaker version this morning and should be told the real one.**

### 2. The 20:00-22:00 indoor slot is NOT booked

The doc says "Steve Peer, indoors, 20:00-22:00, BOOKED." What Zaal actually said:

> "We have a group of hip-hop artists from Steve Peer that I need to confirm. He said he'd reach out to them this weekend."

So the last two hours of the festival are **a group Steve is still chasing**, not Steve's own set. That is a materially different level of certainty on the closing slot of the day.

### 3. Werb is out because he never replied

> "Werb is not going to be performing. He hasn't responded to me."

The doc records "Werb is out" as a decision. It is an absence, not a verdict. Worth knowing if he surfaces.

### And one thing the doc had right

The fire slot now has a proposed home: **inside Stilo's set, in the 18:00-19:30 window**, with Stilo continuing to DJ and calling people outside for it. Zaal checked sunset live on the call - **Ellsworth sunset on 3 October is about 18:11** - so that window puts fire at dusk, which is when it reads.
