# Dcoop x Zaal - ZAOstock AV, travel and the artist rider

**Date:** 2026-08-31 (Monday), about 10.6 minutes
**Attendees:** Zaal, Dcoop, Matt (matjamez - present, never spoke)
**Platform:** Discord, recorded by Craig as a 3-track multitrack
**Project:** ZAOstock
**Transcript:** [transcript.md](transcript.md) - speaker labels are ground truth from per-track files, not diarization

A short, dense logistics call 33 days out. It resolves the single biggest
unowned job on the day - who runs sound - and it reopens one that was written
down as settled.

## The headline: a paid local sound engineer, and Stilo is not it

The call's real decision. Dcoop opened with the question nobody had answered:

> "will stilo or somebody else be running sound like for the musicians"

Zaal's answer is to hire out, and to pay for it personally if that is what it
takes:

> "I'm willing to pay someone for the like six hours or whatever, especially if
> I'm like going to get someone long term that I can connect with that's local
> here... even if I have to pay someone and it's out of my own pocket, it's well
> worth the extra expense."

Both of them arrived at the same reason independently, which is why this reads
as settled rather than provisional. Dcoop:

> "Stilo wants to be an artist not to be the sound guy... he's performing and
> doing things, he's not sitting there watching the boards and backing up
> everybody else the whole time."

Zaal extends the same logic to Dcoop himself:

> "It's like same thing with you where I wouldn't want you to have to do AV
> because I want you to enjoy the time that you've spent flying up."

**This closes the "Dcoop has no second on AV" item, but not the way it was
framed.** The answer is not a second for Dcoop. It is that neither Dcoop nor
Stilo should be on the boards at all, and a paid local takes the whole day.

### Why it matters beyond convenience

Dcoop made the artist-expectation case, and named names:

> "artists like lion's den and [profit] kind of take that stuff a little more
> seriously when it comes to what they expect out of that... they might have the
> expectation of there's someone that's going to be managing their vocals and
> their instrumentals."

He was explicit that he personally does not need it - "I could probably figure
that out myself" - and that the standard rises with the act. This is a
commitment risk to booked performers, not a preference.

## The DJ is REOPENED, and that reverses a written decision

Zaal, on this call:

> "that's the other thing I need to like really really sort out is like dj
> during the day, um, if we're gonna have one, um, and how we're gonna organize
> that, or at least a person to transition, right? So that the mic can get
> turned on."

Confirmed separately by Zaal the same day: he is **reaching out on Nextdoor to
find local DJs interested in the day.** Nextdoor is a brand new outreach channel
for this event.

**"No DJ outdoors, cut 27 August" is now stale wherever it appears** - the Run of
Show tab, the Standup Aug 31 tab, and any changeover description that reads "MC
plus a sponsor spot" as though it were settled.

Three consequences, none of them resolved here, all of them for Zaal:

1. **The flyer line stays.** Candy's flyer says "DJ and MC between sets". That
   line may become true, so it is NOT to be cut. Separately, as of 2026-08-31 the
   whole flyer package is ON HOLD: Zaal has messaged Candy himself and nothing
   else goes to her until she replies. Anything new for her joins that queue so
   she gets one message back, not a trickle.
2. **The MC rota's rationale weakens.** The recorded reasoning was "with no DJ,
   every changeover is the MC, which is four hours for one voice." If a DJ covers
   the day, the rota may still be right but it is no longer forced.
3. **The five-minute changeovers were sized for an MC handover.** A DJ changes
   what a changeover IS, because music can run under it. That may loosen the
   tightest cell on the grid - the 15:05 slot carrying the event talk, the
   WaveWarZ pitch and a sponsor spot in five minutes.

## Dcoop drives, and brings gear as backup only

He priced it on the call and chose the road:

| Option | Cost | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Fly | "$500 plus" for his ticket alone | rejected |
| Drive | "gas is only be like 200" | chosen |

Zaal asked him to bring gear as insurance, while explicitly capping the ask:

> "It would be great if you could bring stuff as a backup just in case, but I
> also don't want to like ask you to bring a ton of stuff over like 10, 15
> hours."

So Dcoop's rig is the **backup PA, not the primary**. The primary is still
unspecified - see the open questions.

Dcoop has his own kit ready ("I pretty much have my whole setup like organized
again"), including two wireless Sennheisers and the wireless headset mic he uses
for fire spinning, which he is confident hooking up himself. He has never driven
to Maine and wants the drive.

**Budget pressure worth knowing about, though it does not clash with the
festival:** a close friend of Dcoop's partner scheduled a wedding in South
Carolina on **Halloween**, so flights, a dog sitter and the trip all land four
weeks after ZAOstock. Devin is also travelling with Dcoop to Maine, which Zaal
noted "added another trip". None of this conflicts with 3 October. It is why the
drive-versus-fly maths mattered.

## The artist rider - tonight

The most time-boxed item on the call. Zaal wants an advance/rider out to every
artist **tonight**:

> "the rider, the like what I want to send to all of the artists... I need to
> send it out like today or tomorrow."

The mechanism they agreed:

- Dcoop already has one from ZAOville - "I already have the one from ZAOville, we
  could literally just plug and play it", built in a Google Doc.
- They build it live together during the Fractal call, starting after Zaal's 17:00
  parklet meeting and before or during the 18:15 Fractal start. Zaal will call
  Dcoop, who will be in the studio.
- They finalise Dcoop's version first, then format it for every other artist.
- Dcoop is **CC'd on the artist email** as, in Zaal's words, "additional support
  from the festivals team."

Zaal explicitly asked Dcoop NOT to send the template early:

> "I would say don't even send it to me now. I have just too much. Send it to me
> when we do this in a few hours."

That is a deliberate sequencing choice, not an oversight. Do not "helpfully"
chase the template ahead of the call.

## Dcoop's read of the day, unprompted

He had read the organizing doc before the call and played back his understanding,
which matches the current running order:

> "most of the music sets will be outside and will run up until Stilo starts
> DJing around, you know, six-ish, and that's when I'll fire spin in between that
> area."

Consistent with the proposed fire window of 18:00-19:30 inside Stilo's set, and
with sunset at about 18:11.

## The college AV route

Dcoop's suggestion, and Zaal called it "a great idea" on the spot. Local college
AV clubs and music programs, where students need practical credits:

> "he got credits for his class by doing sound at those events, so that might be
> a route too."

This is a second, cheaper channel for the same hire, and it is not yet anybody's
action beyond the Nextdoor push.
