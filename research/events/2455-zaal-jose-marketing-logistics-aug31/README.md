## The two things Zaal named as still unclear

Asked what was on the critical path, he named exactly two:

> "The two biggest things that are unclear are the DJ throughout the day and... the AV system."

**AV** - Steve is helping find local support and it is "not 100% confirmed." Note the conflict inside that: Steve is both the person sourcing AV and the drummer in the first band.

**The DJ** - and Zaal is specific about what the role actually is:

> "The DJ outside for the first six hours is the thing I still need to finalize. And I need them to be really smooth at, like, toss us the mic and while we're in between so we can talk, and while we're talking you can set up the next person."

That is not a music role. It is the changeover mechanism. Which is why it interacts with the MC rota and the five-minute changeovers.

Event insurance was named as the only other critical-path item.

## Three pieces of new work Jose surfaced

### 1. Transportation on the day - nobody had planned it

> "Nothing here is easy access without a car."

Zaal has a contact who could drive people locally if needed, and expects Fellenz and some artists to have cars, and the distance is under a mile. But there is no plan. His own words: **"Actual transportation on day of with vehicles. I do need to plan that. So thank you."**

Jose's framing, from running events:

> "Transportation, water and coffee. That's like the religion of production here. You have to guarantee at least water, coffee and the transportation, because sometimes you don't have anything else to offer to your production team."

Water is already covered - Black Moon is providing bottled water at both stages. Coffee and transport are not.

### 2. The team should be visibly a team

Jose asked whether the crew has anything that marks them as one unit - "a uniform or some kind of stuff that labels the team as a cell, a single cell." Zaal: **"That's a good idea. I actually didn't have it full planned, but I'm going to write that down."** Staff/organiser merch.

This also solves a problem already on the board from a different direction: Black Moon's dressing room is performers-only and needs wristbands or lanyards to be enforceable.

### 3. Publish the run of show

Jose wanted it for attendees AND for online - "it's great to have that for the people online so you can share with a friend." The run of show currently exists as an internal grid and a screenshare deck, not as anything a member of the public can read.

## Jose's craft notes, worth keeping

**On the DJ, via a wedding that went wrong.** He watched a "professional" DJ play songs about weddings ending badly, at a wedding. His point: reading the room is not something you can assume you bought.

> "People that read the room - that is something you can get from somebody, but you need to have somebody who reads the resources that you gave him about how the room is going to be that day."

So: give the DJ a brief and a playlist, built with Hurricane and Fellenz, of **songs the artists will NOT perform live**. Let the DJ keep their own agenda inside that frame. And the ambitious version - if the DJ can take the live feed and work with it, "something magical."

**On AV team size:**

> "Two people are great. If you have three you are blessed. More than that you are greedy."

With three, he suggests: one married to the musicians on stage, one roaming for real-time moments, one with the logistics team catching the behind-the-scenes.

**On incentives rather than influencers.** Jose's restaurant example: instead of paying influencers, invite "influent people" - people whose family and friends will come because they did. The incentive is the invitation.

**On planning a day that will change anyway:**

> "It's important to have drafted a vision on paper that then, because of that dynamism of the day, just changes subtly."

Either the $20 is real and the page is incomplete, or it was thinking aloud. **Unresolved and it blocks nothing, but the page ships a price.**

- **"I don't want to commit to any of that."** On rewards for paid tickets: "if we have an easy, fun way for us to give a reward, we will do it. But I don't want to commit to any of that because it's not part of the MVP." **But `/donate` already publishes two perks** - a 1:1 with the ZAO team, and supporter credit. This is the third time in one day the same gap has surfaced from a different direction.

- **The closing act is further along than the doc says.** The Standup Aug 31 tab records it as a group Steve "still needs to confirm." Here Zaal says Steve is **paying for them** and finalising. Still not booked, but the commitment is firmer than recorded.

## The working relationship, and what it needs

Zaal sent Jose the whole organizing doc and was direct about what it is:

> "This document is more my AI notes of my organization of the thing... It's not for you to read, Jose. It's for you to put into your AI. The intent is not for you to read. It's a database of all the information."

Jose's reaction - "it's like a whole forest, oh my god, we can get lost here" - is the honest response to a 14-tab doc, and it is why Zaal has been building a separate packet for humans.

**A real blocker sits underneath the photo work.** Jose has about 160GB of storage left and cannot take full-resolution copies of the archive. Paper has not answered about the links, and the suggestion so far was to copy actual content rather than link it, which Zaal called "not very helpful, because it takes storage then." Jose needs a numbered reference so he can resume from where he stopped; he has five or six more pictures to find.
