## The date nobody had written down

At 25:38 Zaal, Paper and Zach work out between them that **Monday 7 September is
Labor Day**. Checked against the calendar: correct, and it is the first Monday of
the month.

Nothing in the ZAOstock repo or the vault mentions Labor Day. It is the same day
as **the automatic lineup reveal** and **the Arbor Camp site visit**, which means:

- The reveal lands on a US federal holiday, which is a poor day for press pickup.
- The Arbor Camp visit assumes a business is staffed on a holiday Monday.
- The working week for sending artist memos effectively starts Tuesday 8
  September.

A related correction: several documents outside this repo described 7 September
as a **Sunday**. It is a Monday.
