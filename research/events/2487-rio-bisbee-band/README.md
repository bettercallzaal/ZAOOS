## Findings

**What it is.** Rio Bisbee.Band (styled "Rio Bisbee.Band" on their own site; "Rio Bisbee Band" everywhere else) formed in 1980 on the banks of the Bisbee River in Camden, Maine, as a contradance orchestra (fiddle, guitar, banjo, concertina, trumpet, bass) playing thirteen gigs that first year. It shed the folk instruments within a year or two and became a rock band. It has run continuously since — 45-46 years by their own count — and describes itself as "anarcho-syndicalist in operation": no fixed bandleader, a rotating cast of players who take on departmental roles (music director, sound, promotion, etc.), and an explicit ethos against "remaking commercially approved sounds for marketing purposes."

**Genre.** Self-described "Rock Pointillism" — a power-trio core (guitar/bass/drums, guitarist on lead vocal) that expands with whoever's around: the band's own copy lists African, blues, cajun, country, flamenco, funk, jazz, klezmer, Middle-Eastern, polka, punk, reggae, rockabilly, ska, soca, thrash, western and zydeco as source styles pulled into original material. Their BandMix genre tags are narrower: Jazz, Reggae, Latin, Rock. Over 80 original compositions, catalogued on their own site's song list (titles from "455 Revival" to "World War/Global Love").

**Current lineup and size.** Per their BandMix profile (posted 19 Aug 2024, so ~13 months old as of this research — flag as dated): core members are Harrison Maheu (guitar), Yosarian (bass), Steve Arineau (drums) and Mike Hamele (percussion), actively seeking a vocalist, horns, keys, pedal steel or violin. Historically over 45 years the band's own "Meet the Bisbees" page lists 60+ musicians who have rotated through across vocals, drums, percussion, guitar, banjo, keyboard, woodwinds/brass and dance roles (GigSalad's shorter bio says "50+"). This variable-size format is the main scheduling risk for a tightly-clocked single-stage day like ZAOstock's.

**Location and touring radius.** Home base is Studio 455 at 455 North Street, Ellsworth, Maine — inside the same town as ZAOstock's Franklin Street Parklet venue. GigSalad lists the band as traveling up to 120 miles from Ellsworth (covers Bangor, Bar Harbor/MDI, Bucksport, and most of Down East and Mid-Coast Maine, not beyond). No evidence anywhere in the researched sources of touring outside Maine; the historical gig-gallery captions (Waldoboro Days 1982, Ducktrap 1982, Snow Bowl 1985, Geno's [Rock Club, Portland] 1986, Featherfoot Farm 2015) show a decades-long Maine bar/festival circuit, not national touring, plus recurring airplay on WERU-FM and WMPG Community Radio.

**Size / stage of career.** This is a legacy hyper-local act with essentially no current mainstream digital footprint: their YouTube channel (@riobisbeeband2886) shows 43 subscribers, 23 videos and 3,506 total views, joined Dec 2016 — measured live on this fetch. SoundCloud tracks are hosted on former/current member Harrison Joseph Maheu's personal account, not a dedicated band account. Their own site links a Facebook *search* rather than a stable page; a web search turned up two separate Facebook pages (`facebook.com/riobisbeeband` and `facebook.com/newbisbeebandpage`), suggesting the band's own social presence is fragmented — Facebook fetch attempt failed (see Sources). GigSalad lists booking as "Contact for rates" with no price posted, and no reviews on file yet. This is a low-leverage promotional partner compared to acts with an active following, but that is consistent with several already-booked ZAOstock acts, which are also small local artists rather than acts with a national audience.

**Direct connection to ZAOstock already in place.** This is the strongest single fact for fit: per the band's own 2026 event calendar, on **29 August 2026** they played Black Moon Public House in Ellsworth as part of "BBY Productions and Studio 455 Present: Equinox Music and Arts Jamboree and Harvest Luau," alongside Selim Ucar, Tim Seeley, Hymn For Her and Mood. Black Moon Public House is ZAOstock's confirmed partner and official after-party host (`src/content/site.ts` PARTNERS array, role "The evening, and the official after-party," confirmed: true), and per the 15 Sep 2026 Iman x Zaal call, Black Moon's Steve Peer is one of ZAOstock's three shared stage managers alongside Zaal and DCoop. In effect, ZAOstock's own after-party venue has already booked this band once this year.

**Tone / editorial fit.** The band's own copy leans deliberately weird and countercultural ("orgiastic confluence of talent and madness," "resisting empire advocating profit-free fair trade," song themes explicitly including drug references — e.g. "I Remember Quaaludes," "Reefer Leave Me"). None of it is web3/crypto-adjacent, which lines up cleanly with ZAO's public "digital creators, no crypto language" rule (`feedback_no_crypto_language.md`) — there's simply nothing to scrub. But the band's own voice is edgier than ZAOstock's family-facing community-festival copy, so any public billing would need light framing (bio pulled from the music/history angle, not the "resisting empire" language) rather than a direct lift of their site copy.

**Their own 2026 calendar** (self-published, useful for scheduling any future conversation): 21 March (Vernal Equinox fire gathering), 2 May (Revival! Music Festival), 21 June (Summer Solstice fire gathering), 29 August (Black Moon Public House jamboree, above), and a fire/harvest gathering at Studio 455 in September — their site lists this event as both "19 September 2026 3 pm" and, in a separate line, "21 September Autumnal Equinox Fire Gathering"; the two dates don't reconcile and are presented as-is (their own site inconsistency, not this doc's error).
