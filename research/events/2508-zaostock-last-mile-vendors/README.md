## Findings

### 1. Portable restrooms

**Sizing, done with the vendor's own published rule:** Ray Plumbing & Heating publishes an "Estimating Your Needs" chart on their own event-planning page. **Minimums and method.** General industry figures found (not vendor-quoted for this specific order): most Maine screen-print shops set minimums in the **12-to-24-piece** range per design; one comparison shop in this same search, Maine Promotional (the Sunset, ME sister brand above), is reported to require 24 pieces mixed across sweatshirts/tees under one design. **15 pieces sits below a typical 24-piece screen-print minimum, at or above a typical ~12-piece minimum - a genuine border case that is vendor-dependent, not a flat no.** For a one-off run this small, **DTG (direct-to-garment) or DTF (direct-to-film) is the better method than screen printing**: neither requires the screen-setup/burn cost that only pays off at higher volume, and both handle full color at the same price as one color. Ask each vendor to quote DTG/DTF specifically, not screen printing, at 15 pieces. [PARTIAL - general industry figures, not this order's actual vendor quotes]

**Call script:**
1. "I need 15 crew sweatshirts - hoodies or crewnecks, one design, one or two colors or full color - for an event October 3rd. What's your per-piece price at 15, and do you recommend DTG/DTF or screen printing at that quantity?"
2. "What garment brands/colors do you stock, and what's turnaround from approved art to pickup?"
3. "Can you deliver by October 1st or 2nd if I place the order by September 24th?"
