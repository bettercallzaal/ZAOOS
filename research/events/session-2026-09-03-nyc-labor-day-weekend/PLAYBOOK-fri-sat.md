## Friday

**Anchor:** Solana Labs, 141 E Houston, early afternoon, time controlled by Gabe.

**The corridor rule.** Until Gabe texts, stay inside **LES / East Village / SoHo /
Lower Manhattan / Williamsburg.** From anywhere in that zone you are under 25
minutes from the door. Midtown is 40+ and is how you miss the only day he has.

**In the building — three asks, in this order:**
1. **"Who should I meet while I'm here?"** — ask Gabe *before* you arrive, not at
   the door. Intros do not happen by showing up.
2. **The ecosystem coworking desk.** Solana reserves ~75% of that space for
   ecosystem teams to cowork free on approval. That is Rung 1 of ZAO NYC with a
   free venue attached. Ask how approval works.
3. **The ZAO NYC flag.** First time saying it out loud outside the ecosystem.

**Candy.** She is in this city and you have not set a time. A founding
conversation in a room beats one over text next week, and your window closes
Sunday noon.

**Evening:** a Brooklyn event is cheap tonight *only* because you are collecting
the bag anyway. Sequence: `Brooklyn event → bag → PATH → Jersey City`.
A Manhattan event tonight costs ~30-40 min of backtracking.

## Saturday

**Full day, Jersey City base, Manhattan-biased and PATH-anchored.** FiDi, Tribeca,
SoHo, West Village, Chelsea, Flatiron are all a 15-25 min ride home. A Brooklyn
event tonight costs 75-90 minutes at 1am — **this is the night to cut Brooklyn**,
unlike Friday.

**Soft curfew.** Noon Sunday departure means up by 8-9am. Saturday is your last
real night but not an all-nighter.

**Saturday is the depth day.** Friday is anchored by Solana and fragmented by
waiting on Gabe. Saturday is the day to actually sit with two or three people
rather than touch ten. If Candy has not happened by Saturday, make Saturday
Candy's day.

## Capture — the part that decides whether any of this counted

Nine names, two days, loud rooms. The failure mode is not missing events. It is
getting home Sunday with a phone full of handles and no memory of who was who.

- **One line per person, same day.** Name, where, what they do, what you said you'd
  send. Voice memo works if typing doesn't.
- **Send the thing before you sleep.** A promise made at 11pm and kept at 11:30pm
  is worth ten made and kept next week.
- **Your ICM box as a QR on the lock screen** beats typing handles nine times.
- Anyone who says "send me the ZAOstock link" is a **warm lead with 29 days on the
  clock** — capture those separately from everyone else. That list is the actual
  deliverable of this trip.
