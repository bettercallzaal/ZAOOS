## What ZAO NYC does - Zaal picked all of it

Selected 2026-09-03: conference-tethered meetups, a monthly artist session /
showcase, a recurring coworking day, and a ZAO-PALOOZA revival. Plus three
specifics in his own words: *"prep for next year zaopaloza,"* *"prep for getting
nyc onchain artist together with candy,"* and *"plan for a wavewarz."*

That is a roadmap, not a first step. A chapter with no convened roster attempting
four recurring formats at once runs none of them. So it is sequenced below - the
same list, ordered so each rung produces the input the next one needs.

## The ladder

**Rung 1 - Coworking day + conference-tethered meetups. Starts now.**
Cheapest, most repeatable, and the only one that *builds a roster* rather than
requiring one. Conference-tethered is already proven: it is what NFT NYC 2024,
2025 and this weekend already are. Solana Labs at 141 E Houston reserves 75% of
its space for ecosystem teams to cowork free on approval - worth asking about
while Zaal is in that building Friday.
*Produces:* names. Which is the input to everything below.

**Rung 2 - Get the NYC onchain artists together, with Candy.**
Zaal's own phrasing. This is the music-first version and the highest brand fit.
It needs Candy leading and it needs Rung 1's names.
*Produces:* an artist list - the input to Rungs 3 and 4.

**Rung 3 - A WaveWarZ IRL.**
The lowest-risk of the big formats because it has run twice in New York already:
ZAO-PALOOZA 2024 and the Lil WaveWarZ Takeover 2025. Doc 1231: *"WaveWarZ can
export its battle format to any physical venue."* Needs artists (Rung 2) and a
room (Rung 1).
*Produces:* proof the format still works, with a current roster.

**Rung 4 - ZAO-PALOOZA at NFT NYC 2027.**
The flagship, and the thing the other three are secretly for. Precedent is
strong: 2024 ran **12 artists on a 6-week lead time with a 6-person volunteer
team and broke even.**
*Needs:* everything above, plus a budget decision and a venue.

**The runway is not the constraint.** If NFT NYC 2027 lands around the same time
of year, that is roughly twelve months out, against a six-week build last time.
**The constraint is roster, which is why Rung 1 starts now and not in spring.**
