## The rule

**A member is anyone where OG balance > 0 OR ZOR (id 0) balance > 0, on at least one of their Ethereum addresses.**

- Threshold is "more than zero", because Zaal said "anyone with". No minimum is set. Changing it later is one constant.
- Both tokens count, because the ICM box defines Respect as both. A ZOR-only holder (34 of them by the July count) would be locked out by an OG-only check.
- Respect is soulbound, so a balance cannot be bought or borrowed to get in.

## How the check runs at sign-in

1. The member signs in with Farcaster (SIWF). That gives the FID and the custody address.
2. The server looks up the FID's verified Ethereum addresses (a Farcaster hub, or Neynar if the app holds a key).
3. The server does one viem `multicall` on Optimism: OG `balanceOf(addr)` and ZOR `balanceOf(addr, 0)` for the custody address and every verified address. This is the same call shape as `src/app/api/proposals/vote/route.ts:79-88`.
4. Any address with a balance over zero lets them in, and the session records `isMember: true` with the time it was checked.
5. **A failed read is "unknown", never "not a member".** If any call in the multicall fails, sign-in shows "could not check your Respect, try again" and does not tell them they are not a member. ZAOOS learned this already: `src/lib/respect/onchainBalances.ts` explains that a failed read once zeroed members' cached Respect, and the vote route refuses with a 503 rather than count a failed read as zero.
6. The check runs again when the session expires. Respect does not leave a wallet, so this only affects someone who has removed a verified address.

**What is checked is the member's own identity addresses, never the XMTP burner address.** The burner key is generated by the app and holds nothing, so a balance read on it would always be zero. The burner model in the README does not change.

## What this changes in the plan

- The rewrite of `/api/members` in the README's copy-vs-rewrite table becomes the Respect check above. The app needs no copy of the ZAOOS allowlist, which removes option A's import step.
- The app still needs a small table of `fid -> xmtp address` for members who have signed in, so they can find each other in the directory. That is a list of who has used the app, not the authority on who is a member: the chain is.
- Someone who holds Respect but has never opened the app will not show in the directory until they sign in once. Listing every holder up front would mean indexing mint events on both contracts. That is not needed for day one.
- New config: an Optimism RPC URL. The public `https://mainnet.optimism.io` is what ZAOOS uses today; whether its rate limit is enough for sign-in traffic was not measured.
