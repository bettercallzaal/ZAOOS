## ARTICLE DRAFT: "117 Weeks of Showing Up: The ZAO Fractal and What Unbroken Governance Looks Like"

*This article is meant for the ZAO community, Farcaster, and anyone building in the Web3 music space who cares about governance that actually works.*

---

### We started a Monday meeting and didn't stop.

Every Monday at 6pm ET, a group of musicians, builders, and community members gets on a call. We split into random circles of up to six people. Each person presents something they contributed to The ZAO that week — a song, a build, a community action, an event. The group ranks each other's contributions through consensus. No voting blocs. No token-weighted ballots. Just honest conversation about who did what and who is showing up.

Then we go onchain.

The results are submitted to the OREC contract on Optimism Mainnet. Every session's rankings are an immutable public record — who was there, what they contributed, what the group thought of it. The accumulated Respect converts to ZOR, our governance token. When proposals come up, ZOR holders vote on Snapshot. The circle is closed.

We have now done this **117 times in a row.**

---

### Why 117 matters

As of August 2026, The ZAO has run 110 consecutive weekly Fractal sessions. We started with a small circle of artists who wanted governance that couldn't be bought. By Week 117 — arriving in early October, right alongside ZAOstock, our first full music festival — we will have completed over two years of unbroken community coordination.

No week skipped. No quorum failure. No plutocratic takeover.

That is remarkable, and we want to mark it.

The running streak is now documented as **among the longest unbroken DAO governance streaks in Web3 music**. We know of no other music-focused DAO that has achieved this. That's not a boast — it's an observation that weekly fractal governance is harder to maintain than it looks, and the fact that this community kept showing up is worth naming.

---

### What the fractal actually is (for the uninitiated)

The ZAO uses **Optimism Fractal**, an open governance protocol from the Optimism ecosystem. It has four properties that separate it from most DAO governance:

**1. Non-plutocratic.** Your voice is not proportional to how many tokens you hold. It's proportional to how much you contribute, as judged by your peers over time. Early contributors have more Respect — but only because they've been showing up longer and being seen doing it, not because they bought more at launch.

**2. Onchain and verifiable.** Every session result is submitted to the OREC contract (`0xcB05F9254765CA521F7698e61E0A6CA6456Be532` on Optimism Mainnet). No one can retroactively alter the record. Any researcher, community member, or governance analyst can pull the full history.

**3. Snapshot-integrated.** For formal proposals — token distributions, protocol changes, partnership commitments — ZOR holders vote through Snapshot (`snapshot.org/#/zaofractal.eth`). The fractal provides the Respect that eventually becomes ZOR. ZOR provides the vote weight that decides formal questions.

**4. Built for music.** The ZAO fractal is specifically focused on music contributions: tracks released, shows performed, streams hosted, beats shared, artists supported. We are not a generic DAO that happens to have a music channel. Governance here reflects who is actually making music culture happen.

### The ZOR ledger (what the onchain record shows)

Two token contracts track the history:

- **OG ZOR (ERC-20):** `0x34cE...` — the original governance token. Supply frozen at 38,484. Accumulated across Weeks 1-67. This is the vote weight for formal proposals.
- **ZOR Awards (ERC-1155):** `0x9885CCeEf7E8371Bf8d6f2413723D25917E7445c` — the active awards token. 288 live awards, 16,418 ZOR across Weeks 68-110. Earned by participating.

Every week's distribution is a permanent record of community health. The ledger shows who is still here, who has been recognized by their peers, and how recognition compounds over time for those who keep contributing.

### Snapshot + OREC: how governance decisions happen

When something formal comes up — a partnership, a treasury allocation, a protocol change — the process is:

1. **Proposal submitted** to the Snapshot space (deadline: at least 3 days before the vote closes)
2. **ZOR holders vote** with their OG ZOR weight (72h voting window)
3. **Veto window** (72h after vote closes — any holder with 1,000+ Respect can veto)
4. **Result finalized** and executed through OREC

The minimum vote weight to participate in governance is 1,000 Respect (~2.6% of total OG supply). The veto threshold is the same — keeping the minority voice audible without blocking routine decisions.

At **ZAOstock (October 3, 2026)**, we will hold the largest IRL fractal governance session in ZAO history. Remote participation will run simultaneously. Proposals will be open for submission through September 28.

### Week 117: what we're celebrating and what comes next

When we reach Week 117, we will be celebrating:

- **2+ years of unbroken community governance** — showing up when it was uncomfortable, when attendance was low, when the market was down, when the season didn't favor us
- **The ZOR ledger as a verified social contract** — every distribution on record, no retroactive manipulation, no whales overriding the community's judgment
- **Governance that works at ZAOstock scale** — the first time our fractal runs inside a music festival, with IRL participants physically present alongside remote contributors

What comes next: upgrade proposals are on the table. Multi-respect leaderboards (separating code contributions, music contributions, and support contributions into distinct visibility tracks). The camera-on award, automated instead of manual. A 4-week break test for members who need to step away without losing standing. These are proposals for the community to decide — and the fractal is how we'll decide them.

### How to participate

**New to The ZAO:** The fractal is open to anyone contributing to the ZAO ecosystem. To understand what counts as a contribution, read the OREC guide or ask in the ZAO Discord/Telegram. To earn Respect, you need to be present and presenting.

**Building in Web3 governance:** The full session history is on Optimism Mainnet. OREC is auditable at `0xcB05F9254765CA521F7698e61E0A6CA6456Be532`. The ZOR contracts are public. Researchers welcome.

**Coming to ZAOstock:** Proposals for the October 3 session must be submitted by September 28 to be included in the governance agenda.
