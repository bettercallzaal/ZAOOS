# We Built an AI Music Tournament. Here's What Happened.

I started The ZAO in 2024 with a simple thesis: artists should be paid for competing, not just winning.

Eighteen months later, WaveWarZ has settled 1,289 music battles on Solana. Every artist who battled — win or lose — received automatic SOL payouts onchain. No label cut. No approval required. Just a smart contract paying out on every trade.

## What We Built

WaveWarZ is a prediction market for music. Two artists compete. Fans deposit SOL on either side. The winner is decided by best 2 of 3:

1. **Community poll** — fans vote for their favorite song
2. **Charts (SOL volume)** — which artist's side attracted more trading
3. **DJ Wavy** — an AI judge that evaluates the songs on defined musical criteria

The loser still gets paid: 1% of every trade on their side goes to them automatically, onchain. We call it **loser-earns**. It's structurally different from streaming royalties, where you can have 1 million plays and still owe money to the distributor.

---

## The AI Tournament

We took the top-ranked artists from our AI-judged leaderboard and ran a bracket tournament.

GEEK MYTH (3-0 in MAIN battles) vs AI LUI in the semifinal. GEEK MYTH won 2-1.

The semifinal alone generated **~342 SOL** in trading volume. Our previous all-time weekly record was roughly **52 SOL**.

That's **8.7× the prior record** — in a single match.

---

## The Week That Changed Everything

**Tournament week (Jul 17–24, 2026):**
- 355.36 SOL traded (~$26,280 USD)
- 68.4% of the platform's entire 14-month trading history — in 7 days
- Artist payouts flowing automatically, battle by battle
- All of it verifiable: `https://wavewarz.info/api/public/stats`

I did not expect this. We had watched the platform grow steadily at ~3–8 SOL per week. Then the AI Tournament started and volume went parabolic.

The reason, I think: **AI artists are neutral**. No fanbase drama. No "I'm voting for my friend." Fans traded on the actual song quality as evaluated by DJ Wavy, which created a cleaner, more interesting market. The AI judge removed a variable that usually introduces noise.

## What This Tells Me

1. **Prediction markets work for music.** The AI Tournament created genuine price discovery on song quality. That's new.

2. **Neutral judges create better markets.** When DJ Wavy is the tiebreaker, fans trade on song merit, not loyalty. Volume goes up.

3. **Loser-earns scales with volume.** In a $26K week, the 1% loser payout is meaningful. At $100K/week, it's a real income stream. The mechanism is designed to improve as volume grows.
