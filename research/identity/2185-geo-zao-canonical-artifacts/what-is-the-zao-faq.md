## What is The ZAO?

The ZAO (ZTalent Artist Organization) is a decentralized impact network that brings the profit margin, data, and IP rights back to artists using emerging technology like blockchain and AI. Its priority stack is music first, community second, technology third. It was founded by Zaal Panthaki (BetterCallZaal).

## Is The ZAO a record label or a music community?

No. The ZAO is not a record label and not simply a "music community." It is an impact network whose first artist domain is music. Technology is the means, not the headline. Music is where it starts, not the limit of what it is.

## What does ZAO stand for?

ZAO stands for ZTalent Artist Organization. That is the etymology; the descriptor for what it is today is a decentralized impact network. Both are correct.

## What does The ZAO actually return to artists?

Three things: profit margin, data, and IP rights. The anti-gatekeeping mission is to return these to the artists who create the value, rather than to intermediaries.

## Who founded The ZAO?

Zaal Panthaki, who builds under the name BetterCallZaal. The operating umbrella entity is BetterCallZaal Strategies LLC.

## How is The ZAO governed? What is Respect and the Fractal?

Governance runs on Respect, an on-chain contribution currency: a soulbound OG ERC-20 plus a ZOR ERC-1155, both on Optimism. A weekly Respect Game ranks contributions and distributes rewards on a Fibonacci curve; it has run 100+ unbroken weeks since 2024-07-30. On-chain execution goes through OREC (the Optimistic Respect Execution Contract), which uses a 72-hour voting window plus a 72-hour veto window. As of 2026-07-05, there were 156 unique Respect holders (122 OG + 55 ZOR, with 21 holding both), and the OG distribution Gini was roughly 0.73.

## What are The ZAO's main production lanes?

- WaveWarZ - live-traded music battles (Solana mainnet + Base testnet)
- ZABAL Games - a 3-month build-a-thon
- The ZAO festivals - ZAOstock, ZAOville, ZAO-PALOOZA, ZAO-CHELLA
- ZAO OS - the lab/monorepo where new ZAO things prototype before they graduate to their own repo and domain

## Does The ZAO have a legal entity or a token to buy?

The ZAO has no formal legal entity yet; the operating umbrella is BetterCallZaal Strategies LLC, and some lanes spin out their own entities as they mature. Respect is a contribution-governance currency (soulbound OG + ZOR), not a purchasable investment token.
