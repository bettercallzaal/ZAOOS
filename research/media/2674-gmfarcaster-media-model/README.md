# 2674 - GM Farcaster as a model for The ZAO's media: what they do, what we copy, what we skip

> **Goal:** Measure how GM Farcaster runs its show, newsletter and YouTube, set it against The ZAO newsletter, BCZ YapZ and @bettercallzaal, and turn the gap into a ranked list of changes that serve The ZAO, money or YouTube.

## Key Decisions / Recommendations

| # | Decision | Recommendation | Serves |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Newsletter cadence and shape | USE a weekly roundup with a hook title, "The ZAO last week: <hook>", published on a fixed day, with the same section order every week. GM Farcaster has published 143 posts since 2023-11-10, and 83 of them went out on a Sunday (UTC). Ours is titled daily ("Year of the ZABAL - Day N") but shipped 30 posts in January 2026, 1 in May and 3 so far in October. A reliable weekly beats an unreliable daily. This changes the Year of the ZABAL series, so it is Zaal's call. | The ZAO |
| 2 | Every BCZ YapZ episode gets an episode block | USE their per-episode block in the newsletter and the YouTube description: hook title, one-line TLDR, a two-sentence summary, the YouTube embed, chapter timestamps. bczyapz.com already holds chapters and topic tags for episodes (EP19: 12 chapters), so the data exists. | YouTube |
| 3 | YouTube packaging | USE hook titles in their pattern ("Hook, Hook & Hook: BCZ YapZ ep21 with <guest>") instead of "BCZ YAPZ w/Gabriel from Found Some", and cut 3 to 4 topic clips plus a teaser from each long episode. On 2026-10-05 GM Farcaster put out the Splits episode plus 4 topic clips and 1 teaser. Our last 15 uploads have no clips, and three titles appear twice (the Gabriel and Danny episodes each twice on one day, JANGO UU on consecutive days). | YouTube |
| 4 | Fixed "Coming up this week" block | USE a schedule block in every issue: day, time Eastern, show, and where to watch live (twitch.tv/bettercallzaal) or later (youtube.com/@bettercallzaal). GM Farcaster's block sits in every recent issue sampled. | YouTube, The ZAO |
| 5 | A standing "ways to support" list | USE one fixed list near the end of every issue: subscribe on YouTube, follow the channel, subscribe to the newsletter, recast or quote when an episode drops, join the Fractal. Theirs is "7 free things you can do to help support GM Farcaster" and is in every recent issue sampled. | YouTube |
| 6 | A media kit for BCZ YapZ and the newsletter | DRAFT a one-page media kit with measured, honest numbers and a small set of packages. GM Farcaster sells Premier at 2 ETH/month (3-month minimum), Spotlight at 1 ETH/month, one episode from 0.16 ETH, events from 1 ETH, and a $5 USDC on-air shoutout. Prices and whether to sell at all are Zaal's (money). | Money |
| 7 | Use GM Farcaster's own shoutout for ZAO moments | USE the $5 USDC on-air shoutout for Fractal launches and big ZAO moments. Zaal already used it: GM Farcaster thanked "@zaal for Shoutouts" in ep394 (9 Oct) and read his ZAOstock thank-you on 13 Oct. Their media kit claims 1.5k to 2k viewers per episode (self-reported, March 2026). Spend stays Zaal's tap. | The ZAO |
| 8 | Post categories | USE 3 to 5 categories on every post. 354 of our 391 posts have none; GM Farcaster tags 139 of 143 (top set: farcaster, gmfarcaster, ethereum, blockchain, nfts, daos, base, degen, clanker). | The ZAO |
| 9 | What to skip | SKIP their AI-written episode summary ("From our ChatGPT Intern"), the merch shop, the multi-show network, a second knowledge agent and a paid membership for now. Reasons are in the copy/skip table. | - |

The YouTube row is the surprise. GM Farcaster's per-video views are low too, median 30. Their reach claim (1.5k to 2k per episode) is across X, YouTube, the mini app and podcast apps, not YouTube alone. So the lesson is not "their YouTube is big". It is that they package every episode several ways and point every surface at the next live show.

## What GM Farcaster does that we do not

1. **One template, every week.** The 2026-10-14 issue runs in this order: a personal open, the next live times and where to watch, follow asks, the paid-shoutout offer, the media kit link, a milestone with a mint, "Coming up this week", then one block per episode, the merch shop, "7 free things you can do to help support", the Hypersub subscription, the hosts' other writing, and a CC-BY licence line plus a "not financial advice" note. The issues from 25 Jan, 11 May, 10 Jul and 20 Sep 2026 carry the same sections, checked by searching each issue for the section markers.
2. **An episode block that sells the video.** Each episode gets a hook title ("Splits, Stablecoins & Socks With Birkenstocks: GM Farcaster ep393 Monday October 5, 2026 with @abram from Splits"), a thank-you to the guest, a TLDR line of 20 to 30 topics, a short summary, the YouTube embed, and chapter links with timestamps.
3. **Clips.** The Splits episode became the full episode plus four topic clips ("Splits: the easiest way to manage onchain assets", and three more) and a teaser, all on 2026-10-05.
4. **Every surface points at the next live show**, with a time and a link, in the newsletter, the cast and the site ("Live 3x Week").
5. **They sell the audience openly**, with a media kit and five priced packages, and a $5 self-serve shoutout that an agent can buy. The shoutout page lists recent paid reads and their dates.
6. **The newsletter goes out natively on Farcaster too.** The @gmfarcaster account casts the newsletter's opening text, then clips and shoutout highlights (5 most recent casts read from a hub).
7. **They tag everything** with categories, and almost every post has a cover.

## Copy or skip

| Their practice | Call | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly hook-titled roundup | COPY | It is the one shape they kept for three years, and they have twice our subscribers |
| Episode block with chapters | COPY | The chapter data already exists on bczyapz.com |
| Topic clips and teasers | COPY | It is how a 30-view channel still shows up in several feeds |
| Schedule block, support list | COPY | Costs one paragraph each, points at YouTube |
| Media kit and packages | COPY the shape, Zaal sets prices | Money; our numbers are smaller, so the kit has to say them honestly |
| "From our ChatGPT Intern" AI summary | SKIP | Our newsletter speaks in Zaal's voice; an AI paragraph reads as botty. Use the transcript's chapters and a line Zaal writes instead |
| Merch shop | SKIP for now | Public design is not AI-made, and merch is not this quarter's money |
| Network of several shows, games, trivia | SKIP | Focus; BCZ YapZ and the Fractal are already two shows |
| Warpee knowledge agent | SKIP | ZOE and Bonfire already hold our transcripts; a second agent would duplicate them |
| Hypersub paid membership | SKIP until measured | The page could not be fetched, so there is nothing to judge |
| CC-BY licence line | Zaal's call | A licence choice, not a format choice |
| Post coins | Already doing | 140 of our 391 posts already carry a coin |
