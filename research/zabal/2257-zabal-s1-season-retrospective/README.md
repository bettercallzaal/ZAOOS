# 2257 - ZABAL Gamez Season 1: the June record

> **Goal:** The primary-source account of what ZABAL Gamez Season 1 actually
> produced, built from the session data itself rather than from summaries. It
> exists to serve two jobs at once: the season's public retrospective, and the
> "evidence of community support" the Fisher Fund application needs by 2026-08-15
> (doc 1372).

## The finding

**Thirty-one different people, from twenty-five organisations, volunteered to
teach inside thirty days.**

That sentence is the season. Not the submission count, not the finals - the fact
that a community with no budget for speakers convened 32 sessions in a single
month, and almost nobody taught twice.

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|

| Date span | 2026-06-01 to 2026-06-30 - all inside one month |
| Format | 31 workshops + 1 fireside |
| **Distinct presenters** | **31** (only `yerbearserker` presented twice) |
| **Organisations represented** | **25** |
| Sessions with a transcript | 29 of 32 |
| Sessions on YouTube | 28 of 32 |
| Track split | builder 22, artist 5, creator 5 |

Corroborating figures from doc 1466 (S1 Completion Report): 32 builders enrolled,
30+ PRs shipped in June, ~80% builder retention through June.

**A note on a number in circulation.** "31 of 34 workshops recorded" appears in
earlier summaries. The session data shows 32 entries, of which 28 carry a YouTube
link and 29 a transcript. The figures in this doc come from the data file; where
they differ from a summary, prefer these and say so.

## Why this is the Fisher evidence

Doc 1372 records the Fisher Fund at 2,000-5,000 USD with a **hard deadline of
2026-08-15**, blocked on a Fractured Atlas fiscal sponsorship application that had
not been started as of 2026-08-09 (tracker task 9249, due 2026-08-11).

Grant reviewers asking for "evidence of community support" are asking whether
anyone other than the founder shows up. The answer here is unusually concrete:

- **31 presenters** who were not paid, teaching a public cohort
- **25 organisations** lending their people to someone else's programme
- **28 public recordings** - the evidence is not a claim, it is watchable
- **A named cohort of 32 builders** with ~80% retention across the month

That is stronger than most letters of support, because none of it is testimony.
It is a record of what people did.

## What July says, and why it belongs in the retrospective

July stalled. Doc 1466 records it plainly: "0 confirmed" July sessions, no recaps,
no daily updates in the data files. The site's own daily-updates file holds four
entries, the most recent dated 2026-07-24.

This belongs in the public account rather than being edited out. A season that
ran hot for a month and then paused is a real thing that happens to volunteer
programmes, and saying so is what makes the June numbers credible. A
retrospective that reports only the good month invites the reader to wonder what
was left out.

## The four gaps, and what they have in common

Doc-level counts say 28 of 32 sessions have a YouTube link and 29 of 32 a
transcript. Named, the gaps are:

| Date | Session | Format as recorded | Has |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-06-06 | Bonfires + a vibe-coding masterclass (Carlos / Plat0x) | Live X Space fireside, recorded | transcript, no video |
| 2026-06-20 | Farcaster Batches, and the builders behind it (Jub Jub) | Farcaster Space fireside, recorded | neither |
| 2026-06-21 | Selling merch onchain (Minted Merch) | Fireside, recorded | neither |
| 2026-06-22 | ZABAL Gamez AMA with The Farcaster Intern | Audio space fireside / AMA, recorded | neither |

**Every gap is an audio space. Every regular workshop has video.** The first read
of this data was that three consecutive days had failed - that is wrong. June 20
carried three sessions, two of them fully captured; June 21 carried two, one
fully captured. The variable is FORMAT, not date.

That matters because all four records say **"recorded"** in their own format
field, and each has a live page (`/recordings/15`, `/19`, `/20`, `/fireside/1`).
So this is very likely a publishing gap rather than lost material: Farcaster and X
Space audio was captured and never pushed to YouTube or transcribed.

Four sessions, four presenters, one fixable cause. Worth closing before the
retrospective is cited to a funder - or, if the audio is genuinely gone, worth
saying so in the same breath as the 28 that exist.
